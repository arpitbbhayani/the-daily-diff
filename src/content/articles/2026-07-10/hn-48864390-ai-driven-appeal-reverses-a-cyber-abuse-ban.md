A user was banned by OpenAI for "cyber abuse," but an AI agent diagnosed the issue, wrote the appeal, and submitted it, resulting in approval by another AI. This is not science fiction; it happened.

The user simply pasted the ban notice into Codex, which then identified the root cause: asking for an API key to his own server. The AI then drafted and submitted a concise appeal.

The entire process-ban, diagnosis, defense, and pardon-was completed by AI systems in about ten minutes. This demonstrates a fascinating and slightly unsettling loop of AI interacting with AI for administrative tasks.

For senior engineers, this highlights the incredible potential of AI agents not just for code generation, but for complex, multi-step problem-solving and automated interaction with other digital systems. It signals a shift in operational paradigms.

AI agents are already navigating the bureaucratic landscape of other AI systems.
