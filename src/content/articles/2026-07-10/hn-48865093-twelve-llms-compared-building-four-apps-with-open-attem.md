Ever wondered how today's top LLMs actually stack up when building real applications?

This experiment put twelve models, including GPT-5.6, Grok 4.5, and several open-weights, to the test on four distinct app-building tasks. Each model got five attempts, allowing for a deep dive into consistency and overall success rates.

The findings offer concrete insights into the current code generation prowess of these models, highlighting their strengths and surprising weaknesses. It is a practical benchmark, not just theoretical.

These observations directly inform your choices for integrating AI into development workflows.
