Vim users often pride themselves on efficiency, but how does this legendary editor adapt to the age of AI? This "Ask HN" thread dives into exactly that.

It is not about replacing Vim's core power but enhancing it. The discussion explores practical integrations, from code completion powered by large language models to AI-driven refactoring.

Engineers are sharing their custom setups, plugins, and workflows that seamlessly weave AI assistance into Vim's modal editing paradigm.

This demonstrates how traditional, highly optimized engineering practices can evolve by strategically adopting new AI capabilities, further boosting developer productivity.

If you are a Vim user or an engineer keen on optimizing your workflow with AI, this thread offers concrete ideas and community wisdom.
