Apple has sued OpenAI, accusing it of "trade secret theft" to build consumer hardware. This is not just a legal spat; it signals a brutal fight for control in the AI hardware future.

Recall the high-profile partnership between Apple and OpenAI just two years ago? That relationship has apparently soured as OpenAI pushed into hardware.

The lawsuit alleges coordinated theft "at every level," from technical staff to chief hardware officers. This legal battle could reshape the landscape for AI product development and hardware integration.

It highlights the immense value companies place on IP in the race to embed AI deeply into consumer devices.
