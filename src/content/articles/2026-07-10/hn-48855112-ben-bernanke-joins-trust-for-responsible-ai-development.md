Anthropic just appointed Ben Bernanke, former Federal Reserve Chair, to its Long-Term Benefit Trust. This independent body oversees the responsible development of advanced AI.

Bernanke is renowned for steering the U.S. economy through the 2008 financial crisis and for his work on the Great Depression. His unique blend of economic and crisis management expertise is now being applied to AI governance.

This move highlights the increasing recognition that the economic and societal impacts of AI are profound, requiring expert oversight beyond purely technical considerations. It demonstrates a proactive approach to managing the "enormous potential and range of outcomes" that AI presents.

Keeping advanced AI aligned with humanity's long-term interests is not just a technical challenge, but a multi-disciplinary one.
