The true bottleneck for the next wave of AI and quantum computing is not discovery, but manufacturing. We have the materials in labs, but scaling them to production is a monumental engineering challenge that is slowing innovation.

This reveals a profound truth: software is built on hardware, and hardware is built on materials. A seemingly distant problem in materials science directly impacts our ability to advance LLM infrastructure and applied AI at scale.

This is a call for cross-disciplinary thinking in system design. Solving these physical scaling issues is just as critical as optimizing code or algorithms for future technological breakthroughs.
