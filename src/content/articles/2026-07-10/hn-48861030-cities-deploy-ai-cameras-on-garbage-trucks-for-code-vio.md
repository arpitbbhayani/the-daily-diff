AI is now rolling through your neighborhood on garbage trucks, scanning for code violations. Cities like Cape Coral and Dallas are deploying AI-powered cameras to automate property inspections.

These systems are designed to detect everything from overgrown lawns to illegal dumping. It is an interesting use case for applied AI, specifically computer vision, moving beyond traditional enterprise applications.

While promising efficiency, these deployments also raise questions about privacy and the expansion of surveillance. Understanding how such systems are implemented and their broader societal impact is becoming increasingly important for engineers.

The future of civic enforcement might just be autonomous and always watching.
