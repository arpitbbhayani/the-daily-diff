China's public QR codes are being hijacked, redirecting users to scams and illicit content. This is a stark reminder that even seemingly simple digital infrastructure can harbor critical vulnerabilities if not managed meticulously.

The issue stems from expired government-registered domains associated with these QR codes. When these domains lapse, they become susceptible to malicious re-registration and redirection, exposing millions of users to risk.

This is a prime example of an oversight in system design and engineering practices, specifically regarding the lifecycle management of digital assets within smart city initiatives. It highlights the profound importance of continuous monitoring and robust operational procedures.

Never underestimate the long-term security implications of infrastructure management.
