Publishers are beginning to block Google's crawlers, a move that could profoundly impact both search quality and AI training. Cloudflare is facilitating this by setting default bot management protocols to block "multi-purpose crawlers" that scrape for both search indexing and AI model training.

This action primarily targets Google, which uses a single crawler for these dual purposes. Publishers are forced into an impossible choice: be discoverable in search or prevent their content from being used to train AI models without consent or compensation.

This is a significant moment for anyone working with applied AI or LLM infrastructure. The availability and sourcing of training data is not just a technical challenge but an economic and ethical one. Expect future AI development to increasingly navigate these complex data access and compensation issues.
