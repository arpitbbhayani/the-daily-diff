The tech workforce is splitting in two, and AI is the wedge. A recent survey from Lenny's Newsletter reveals a stark divide: half of engineers feel amplified and more capable by AI, while the other half feels shaken and less sure of their value.

This bifurcation predicts career sentiment more than seniority or company size. Burnout also jumped 11 points in a single year, with four in ten workers worried about losing their jobs.

Even those optimistic about their own career are hesitant to recommend tech to others. This highlights a critical moment for engineering leadership to navigate this shift thoughtfully.

Are you building skills to be amplified by AI or finding your role challenged? The ground is moving, and understanding these trends is vital for your long-term career strategy.
