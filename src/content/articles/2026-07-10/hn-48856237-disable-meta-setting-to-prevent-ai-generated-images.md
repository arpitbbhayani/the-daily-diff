Meta's new Muse Image AI can generate images of you using your public Instagram profile just by tagging your handle. Many users are unaware their likeness is being used this way.

The company's policy states you will not be notified if someone uses your public content to generate an AI image. This means your digital twin could be out there without you ever knowing.

To opt out, you need to navigate deep into Instagram's settings: Profile > Menu > Sharing and reuse, then toggle off "Posts and Reels." The wording and availability might vary, and opting out only prevents future generation, not existing images.

This feature raises significant privacy concerns and underscores the need for clear consent mechanisms and user control in AI systems that leverage personal data.
