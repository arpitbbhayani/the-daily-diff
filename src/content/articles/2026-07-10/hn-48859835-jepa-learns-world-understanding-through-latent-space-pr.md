Yann LeCun's Joint Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs) are a cornerstone of self-supervised learning. This annotated walkthrough breaks it down completely.

JEPA tackles the challenge of training models to understand the world without explicit labels or falling into trivial solutions. It does this through prediction in representation or latent space.

The article dives into I-JEPA for image understanding and its evolution to V-JEPA for video. It shows how these models learn rich semantic representations by predicting masked regions from visible context.

If you want to understand the architectural mechanics behind how AI can learn meaningful features from raw data without extensive human labeling, this is an excellent resource.
