Meta's AI recommendation systems are incredibly effective at driving user engagement, boosting time spent on Facebook by 8% and Instagram by 6% in a single year. These numbers are staggering, highlighting the immense power of applied AI in shaping user behavior.

This effectiveness, however, comes with a critical ethical dimension. The discussion around "addictive design" and its impact on user wellbeing raises important questions for every engineer building AI systems.

Are we designing for human flourishing or just for metrics? This piece prompts reflection on the engineering practices and accountability needed when deploying AI at scale, especially when system design directly influences billions of lives.
