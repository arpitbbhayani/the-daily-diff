Generative AI is not just for chatbots; it is powering a booming industry of AI-assisted virtual assistants creating "thought leadership" content on LinkedIn. This surprising application highlights the new economic realities of AI.

Virtual assistants in the Philippines are leveraging these tools to produce posts and comments for executives, driving engagement networks. The market for AI-assisted VAs is projected to grow by an astounding 182% in the next decade.

This showcases a human-in-the-loop model where AI amplifies human productivity for specific, often repetitive, content generation tasks. It is a potent blend of human oversight and AI efficiency.

For engineers, this demonstrates applied AI extending beyond traditional tech products into new service economies, reshaping how work is done and perceived. It offers a glimpse into the evolving landscape of digital labor.

AI is quietly transforming service industries, one LinkedIn post at a time.
