The rise of LLMs is sparking a deep sense of unease among many seasoned software engineers. It is more than just learning new tools; it is a fundamental shift in how we approach problem-solving, code generation, and even our own value.

This Hacker News thread captures the raw sentiment: many feel a profound sense of alienation. Questions about job security, the purpose of traditional coding skills, and the future relevance of their expertise are surfacing.

Understanding this sentiment is crucial for leadership and individual contributors alike. We need to foster environments where developers can adapt, reskill, and find new ways to leverage AI, rather than feel replaced by it. Ignoring these feelings is not an option; proactive engagement with the human element of AI's impact on engineering is vital.
