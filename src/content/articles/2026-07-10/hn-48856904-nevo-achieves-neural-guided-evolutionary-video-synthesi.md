Generating videos to precisely stimulate a target brain region is now a reality. This NEvo project uses neural-guided evolutionary synthesis, a sophisticated technique blending AI with biological feedback.

The system does not just create videos; it iteratively refines them based on actual brain responses, pushing the boundaries of what "applied AI" means. Imagine AI systems that directly optimize for human physiological outcomes.

This capability hints at a future where AI agents could tailor experiences for therapeutic applications, or even enhance cognitive functions, moving beyond mere content generation to direct biological interaction.

We are seeing the emergence of AI that understands and manipulates our very biology.
