The discontinuation of an LLM can trigger a cascade of real-world problems for production systems.

Developers are pushing back against Google's plan to deprecate Gemini 2.5 Flash, arguing that its successor, 3.1 Flash Lite, falls short on critical metrics. For instance, 2.5 Flash provides 300-400ms completions, vital for voice agents, while 3.1 Flash Lite can hit 600-700ms or more, effectively breaking these use cases.

Beyond latency, concerns also include performance degradation and a significant 3x cost increase for the new model. This forum thread highlights the practical impact of seemingly minor model changes on developer workflows and deployed applications.

This underscores the critical importance of model stability and predictable performance for applied AI development.
