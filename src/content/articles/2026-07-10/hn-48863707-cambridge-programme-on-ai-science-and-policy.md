Boko Haram is actively leveraging frontier AI technologies, marking a concerning but vital area of study for applied AI. This report sheds light on how advanced AI capabilities are being weaponized in real-world conflicts.

The group is not merely using basic tech; they are adapting sophisticated AI for intelligence gathering, propaganda dissemination, and operational planning. This demonstrates AI's dual-use nature in its starkest form.

For engineers, understanding these unconventional applications is crucial. It underscores the broad, often unforeseen, impact of AI development and the ethical considerations inherent in building powerful AI systems.

AI's application extends far beyond Silicon Valley's typical use cases.
