Decentralized social media protocols, like ActivityPub and ATProto, are often seen as competing. But what if they could work together?

This piece explores a fascinating architectural design provocation: running ActivityPub directly over an AT Protocol Personal Data Server (PDS). This is not about choosing a side, but about finding common ground.

It delves into how ActivityPub's federation model could integrate with ATProto's account portability and data repository structure. Imagine the possibilities for data ownership and interoperability.

The technical sketch highlights that significant interoperability might be achievable with relatively few modifications to existing designs.

This discussion is a valuable thought experiment for anyone invested in the future of open social platforms and robust distributed system designs.
