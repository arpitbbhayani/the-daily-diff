The early days of a high-growth startup are often romanticized, but the reality is brutal focus makes all the difference.

An early Facebook engineer recounts working 120-hour weeks, sleeping with their phone, and babysitting scripts every four hours to keep News Feed running. This level of dedication was driven by an unwavering focus on building the product above all else.

They highlight how Mark Zuckerberg's consistent "no" to anything not directly related to product building, even good causes, was crucial. This principle prevented resource diffusion and maintained conviction in their core mission.

Discover how extreme focus, even amidst scarcity, can define a company's trajectory and elevate engineering output.
