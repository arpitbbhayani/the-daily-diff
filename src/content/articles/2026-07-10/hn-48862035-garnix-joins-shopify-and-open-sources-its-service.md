Garnix, a project focused on Nix-based tooling, is open-sourcing its codebase and the team is joining Shopify. This news marks a significant shift for users of the hosted Garnix service, which will shut down on July 15th, 2026.

The move to open source the Garnix codebase is a positive development for the community, ensuring that the project can live on. Users are encouraged to download their build artifacts before the shutdown date.

For senior engineers leveraging Nix or interested in advanced build systems, this presents an opportunity to explore the Garnix internals. It also highlights the continued industry trend of consolidating developer tooling talent.

This transition underscores the importance of resilient build infrastructure and the impact of team acquisitions on developer ecosystems. The open-sourcing means the project's evolution is now in the community's hands.
