Many believe AI will just write our code for us, but the reality is more nuanced. An attempt to have Claude Code recreate the 1986 game "Thrust" reveals a crucial lesson.

The AI generated "slop" - far from a working game. Yet, the same AI proved invaluable for "software archaeology," dissecting and understanding the *original* disassembled source code.

This highlights a profound truth: current AI excels not at independent creation of complex systems, but as an analytical co-pilot. It processes vast amounts of information to help engineers comprehend.

This changes the narrative from AI replacing engineers to AI augmenting their most complex tasks, like understanding decades-old, poorly documented code.

For engineers navigating legacy systems or optimizing their workflow, leveraging AI for deeper code comprehension is a clear path to enhanced developer productivity.
