Understanding how LLMs reason is a holy grail for AI engineers. Mechanistic interpretability researchers are now applying causality theory to peek inside these powerful models.

This approach promises to demystify why an LLM makes specific decisions, moving us closer to truly reliable and controllable AI agents. Imagine being able to debug an LLM's thought process, not just its output.

This work is foundational for the next generation of transparent and trustworthy AI systems.
