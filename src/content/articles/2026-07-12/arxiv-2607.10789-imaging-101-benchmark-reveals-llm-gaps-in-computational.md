Benchmarking LLM coding agents on real-world scientific problems reveals surprising gaps, and Imaging-101 offers clear insights. This benchmark covers 57 expert-verified computational imaging tasks, canonicalized into a four-stage pipeline.

The study found systematic challenges that go beyond typical coding benchmarks. LLM agents struggle with algorithm selection, handling physical conventions, and properly integrating pipeline components.

Even frontier LLMs face significant hurdles in planning and executing complex scientific code. This highlights that while LLMs can write code, their reasoning for problem decomposition and domain-specific correctness is still maturing.

If you are thinking about agentic AI for scientific or complex engineering tasks, understanding these specific limitations is vital. It informs where human oversight and specialized tools are still indispensable.

Do not underestimate the complexity of scientific reasoning for AI agents.
