Data centers in Ireland now consume a staggering 23% of the nation's electricity, a dramatic increase from just 5% in 2015. This figure even surpasses the consumption of urban households.

This rapid growth has led to an effective moratorium on new data center grid connections in the Dublin area. The energy demand rose 10% in 2025 alone, despite these restrictions.

The environmental and infrastructural pressure caused by our ever-growing digital footprint is immense. It forces engineers and policymakers to rethink how we design, power, and scale our systems.

This situation underscores a critical challenge in building scalable systems and LLM infrastructure: the massive energy cost.
