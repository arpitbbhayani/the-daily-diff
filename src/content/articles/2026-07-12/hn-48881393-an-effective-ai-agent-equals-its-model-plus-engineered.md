“Agent = Model + Harness.” This simple equation captures the critical shift in AI engineering.For senior engineers, the real challenge in building reliable AI agents is less about picking the smartest foundational model and more about crafting the robust scaffolding around it. Think of the harness as the prompts, tools, context policies, sandboxes, and recovery paths that transform a raw model into a functional, reliable agent.A decent model with a superior harness consistently outperforms a great model with a poorly designed one. This insight is borne out by practical experience in developing agentic systems.Focus your engineering effort on building resilient control flows, effective tool orchestration, and intelligent feedback loops. This is where you unlock scalable, production-ready AI agent capabilities.Mastering harness engineering is how you deliver truly effective AI solutions.
