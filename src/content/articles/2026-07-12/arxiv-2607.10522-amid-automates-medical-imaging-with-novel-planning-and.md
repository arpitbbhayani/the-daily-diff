LLM agents are now automating complex medical imaging model development with impressive, auditable results.

Medical imaging tasks have stringent validation protocols and modality-specific experimentation, making them challenging for autonomous machine learning engineering. AMID, an autonomous multi-agent framework, addresses this.

It uses Data-Conditioned Method Planning to refine task search spaces into executable method lanes. Verification-Guided Two-Stage Optimization then moves from broad exploration to selective exploitation, enforcing strict validation throughout.

Across 20 diverse medical imaging challenge tasks, AMID consistently outperformed general-purpose MLE systems. On several tasks, it even matched or approached strong human-designed solutions, producing high-performing and auditable model artifacts.

Agents are transforming bespoke medical imaging engineering into auditable, high-performance workflows.
