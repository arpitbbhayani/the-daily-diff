Writing code or documentation? Ever hit that wall where you know *what* you want to say, but not *how*? That moment of pause can derail your entire flow.

A simple trick from journalism, using "TK" (short for "to come"), can revolutionize your productivity. Instead of stopping to find the perfect word or fact, just type "TK" and keep writing.

This technique helps you maintain momentum, complete the full structure of your thoughts, and then easily revisit the placeholders later. It also acts as an automatic, searchable TODO list in any text editor.

It is a small change that yields significant gains in efficiency, helping you to ship faster and clearer.

Keep your thoughts flowing, effortlessly.
