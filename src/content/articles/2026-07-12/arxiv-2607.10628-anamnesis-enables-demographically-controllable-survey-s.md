Simulating human surveys with LLMs just got a major upgrade. Anamnesis, an open-source platform, uses structured narrative backstories to condition model responses, not just simple persona prompts.

This system can replicate complex demographic behaviors and preferences more accurately than prior methods. For instance, it outperformed baselines in mimicking Pew Research Center's American Trends Panel on political typology and even the New Yorker Caption Contest.

The key insight is moving beyond basic prompting to deeply integrated backstories. This allows for probabilistic demographic resampling and even multimodal surveys.

It means you can prototype and stress-test survey instruments on virtual populations instead of costly human subjects. Think about the implications for market research, policy evaluation, or even game design.

Stop guessing and start simulating with precision.
