Building efficient multi-agent systems that scale without exploding inference costs is a major hurdle. This paper tackles it head-on with GRADE, a hierarchical system that uses lightweight learned gates.

GRADE intelligently governs agent selection, communication, and even the depth of hierarchical traversal. This means fewer active parameters and smarter resource utilization.

The results are compelling: GRADE outperforms strong baselines like MMLUPro by 4.8 points at half the active compute, achieving ~17B average active parameters. This is a significant leap for practical multi-agent deployment.

Consider how an adaptive system like GRADE could reshape your approach to agentic workflows, especially where dynamic resource allocation and cost efficiency are paramount. It is about working smarter, not just harder, with agents.
