Many programmers feel "extinct" today, much like Phil Tippett did when CGI replaced stop-motion in Jurassic Park. This anxiety around AI and LLMs making skills obsolete is palpable.

However, the best way to avoid extinction is to evolve. History shows that new waves of technology create new kinds of work and workers.

The article encourages engineers to "ride the wave," master new tools, and see this as a continuous game of adaptation. It is about learning new tricks, not becoming obsolete.

This perspective is crucial for sustained career growth and effective engineering in the AI era.
