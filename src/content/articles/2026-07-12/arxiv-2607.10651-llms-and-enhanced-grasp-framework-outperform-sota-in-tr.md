Planning multi-day urban travel itineraries is a nightmare of POIs, preferences, and constraints. But what if LLMs could understand your desires, and an optimized algorithm built the perfect trip?

This paper introduces 'Embark Now,' a framework that does exactly that. It integrates Large Language Models to dynamically capture nuanced user requirements with an enhanced Greedy Randomized Adaptive Search Procedure (GRASP) for efficient planning.

Tested on real-world datasets from Beijing and Tianjin, the system significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods. It improves average total itinerary scores by over 4% and 11% across diverse user cases.

Crucially, it achieves these gains with substantial improvements in time efficiency, delivering better plans in shorter computation times. This highlights the power of combining advanced AI with robust optimization.

The future of personalized services lies in intelligent fusion of capabilities.
