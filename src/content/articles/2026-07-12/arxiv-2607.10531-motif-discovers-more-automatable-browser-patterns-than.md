Automating personal web workflows can be proactive and efficient, even for users who do not know what to automate.

Traditional automation relies on users explicitly demonstrating behaviors. Motif, a new system, flips this by passively observing everyday browser activity to discover recurring interaction patterns.

Once a programmable pattern is found, Motif recommends it to the user and, upon confirmation, generates a program. This moves beyond "vibe coding" where users struggle to identify automation opportunities.

In a multi-day study, Motif discovered more automatable patterns than users recognized, with most matching participants' routines and proving useful. This approach streamlines workflow automation without requiring explicit user input or expensive LLM agent calls.

Let your browser tell you what to automate, making productivity seamless.
