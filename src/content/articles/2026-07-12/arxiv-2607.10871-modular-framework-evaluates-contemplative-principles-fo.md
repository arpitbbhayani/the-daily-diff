Achieving ethical alignment in large language models is paramount, especially in sensitive domains. This paper introduces a modular framework designed to systematically evaluate and enhance LLM alignment using principles from contemplative traditions.

The framework provides a plug-and-play pipeline that allows seamless integration of new models, metrics, and benchmarks, making it highly adaptable. This means you can fairly compare different approaches and gain deeper insights into alignment.

It offers a principled pathway for domain experts to define alignment criteria without needing deep technical expertise, making ethical considerations more accessible in LLM development. Initially targeting mental health, its domain-agnostic design makes it relevant for any human-AI collaboration.

This work lays the groundwork for interdisciplinary research bridging cognitive science, philosophy, and system design. It is a step towards building truly robust, trustworthy, and socially beneficial human-AI ecosystems.
