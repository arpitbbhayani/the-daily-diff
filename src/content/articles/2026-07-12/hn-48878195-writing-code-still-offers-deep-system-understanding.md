In 2026, with AI agents generating much of our code, is writing code still essential for engineers?Absolutely.This article makes a compelling case that direct interaction with the execution environment, even with AI assistance, is vital for true architectural understanding.It is about experiencing the system's fragility firsthand, not just observing diffs.Engineers who "feel" the code can better identify weaknesses, clean up inconsistencies, and establish principles that make the "software factory" more robust for agents.This perspective is crucial for senior engineers adapting their role in an AI-driven future, ensuring they remain effective navigators of complex systems.Do not lose touch with the code itself.
