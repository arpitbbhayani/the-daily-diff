This piece argues that today's "useful" companies stand on the shoulders of yesterday's "useless" research. It challenges the conventional view of immediate utility, highlighting that seemingly impractical explorations often become the bedrock for future innovation.

Supporting early-stage, non-obvious research is not a luxury; it is an investment in the next generation of groundbreaking technologies. Understanding this dynamic is key for engineers and leaders aiming to build sustainable, forward-thinking organizations.

We must foster environments where curiosity-driven exploration is valued, paving the way for the next big thing.
