Access to high-resolution household energy data is crucial for smart-grid research, but privacy concerns and collection costs often create a significant bottleneck. WattCouncil introduces an innovative solution: a council of Large Language Model agents.

This framework uses LLM agents operating in specialized roles-generating, auditing, and validating structured energy scenarios-under explicit cultural, temporal, and physical constraints. It is a powerful example of a governed multi-agent system.

Instead of static predictors, these agents act as adaptive decision-makers within a controlled pipeline. They produce context-sensitive daily routines by incorporating factors like household composition, time, and environmental conditions.

The generated profiles are evaluated against detailed real-world datasets, demonstrating their consistency and utility. This approach addresses the data scarcity challenge by creating synthetic yet realistic data with a high degree of fidelity.

Leveraging governed multi-agent LLM systems can solve complex data generation problems, enabling advancements in critical applied AI fields like smart-grid optimization.
