Building robust AI agents often hits a wall with reliable tool use and complex orchestration. Skillscript introduces a declarative, sandboxed language to address this head-on.Think of it as a Makefile for agents: you define "skills" in a structured way, enabling persistent, inspectable automation. This ensures agents execute tasks consistently and safely, moving beyond opaque black-box operations.This approach brings a much-needed layer of control and transparency to agentic systems. It allows engineers to approve what an agent can do and then trust it will run the same way every time, a crucial step for production-grade AI applications.Discover how Skillscript makes agent workflow orchestration reliable and transparent.
