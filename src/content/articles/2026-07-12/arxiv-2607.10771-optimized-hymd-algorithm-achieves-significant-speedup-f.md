Discovering matching dependencies in large datasets for tasks like entity resolution is notoriously slow, but a new approach with Desbordante changes the game. This paper demonstrates a revolutionary 40x speedup, even up to 170x in some cases.The improvements stem from clever optimizations: a new sampling technique, faster generalization lookups, and an improved dependency representation. These are not incremental tweaks; they fundamentally rethink how this computationally intensive problem is tackled.For anyone building data quality pipelines, performing data deduplication, or integrating complex datasets, this is a massive leap forward. It shows that significant performance gains are still possible in foundational data management problems with smart algorithmic and technical innovations.Explore how to achieve lightning-fast data quality with Desbordante.
