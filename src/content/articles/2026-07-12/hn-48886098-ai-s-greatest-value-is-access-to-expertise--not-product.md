Many discussions about AI focus on productivity gains, but what if its true power lies elsewhere? This article posits that AI's biggest unlock is democratizing access to expertise.Imagine a world where specialized knowledge is no longer bottlenecked by human availability or location. AI can serve as a conduit, making deep expertise accessible to a much wider audience, accelerating problem-solving and innovation.This shifts the conversation from merely automating existing tasks to fundamentally changing how we acquire and apply knowledge. It implies a future where AI elevates human capabilities by expanding the reach of specialized skills across organizations.Consider how AI could reshape access to expertise in your organization.
