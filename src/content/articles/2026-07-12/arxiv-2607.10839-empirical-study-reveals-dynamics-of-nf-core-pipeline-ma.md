Maintaining and supporting large, community-driven software ecosystems is a complex challenge, especially in scientific computing. This study offers a deep dive into nf-core, a major Nextflow pipeline ecosystem, by analyzing over 15,000 GitHub issues, 35,000 pull requests, and hundreds of forum discussions.

The findings reveal distinct roles for each platform: GitHub issues primarily capture problem reporting, pull requests handle implementation and updates, and forums serve user-facing support for execution failures and environment-specific issues. Resolution outcomes are strongly linked to factors like actionability, coordination, and the presence of diagnostic evidence.

This research provides practical lessons for engineering leaders on fostering sustainable open-source projects, emphasizing the importance of diverse communication channels and clear processes to enhance developer productivity and community support.
