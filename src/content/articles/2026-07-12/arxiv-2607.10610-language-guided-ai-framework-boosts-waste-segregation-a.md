Efficient waste segregation is a critical challenge for sustainable urban management, but current automated systems often lack contextual understanding and regulatory alignment. What if AI could learn to sort waste based on actual government rules?

WasteAssistant proposes a groundbreaking language-guided vision-AI framework that integrates vision-language models and multimodal Large Language Models for joint visual-linguistic reasoning. It directly aligns with India's Solid Waste Management Rules 2016.

This framework implements a visual question answering paradigm. By leveraging multi-modal LLMs, it achieves impressive results, with a BLIP-based model getting a BLEU score of 0.8291. This significantly outperforms traditional CNN-based methods.

This work demonstrates a powerful path towards deploying multimodal AI in sustainable urban infrastructure, ensuring both high accuracy and regulatory compliance for a vital environmental service.
