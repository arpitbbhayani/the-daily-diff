What if you could achieve the benefits of LLM fine-tuning without actually fine-tuning the model? This paper introduces PTFEA, a framework that theoretically validates and demonstrates the mathematical equivalence between context engineering and model fine-tuning for Multimodal Entity Alignment.

By framing fine-tuning as interpretable context engineering within a curriculum learning framework, PTFEA achieves dramatic efficiency gains. On the ICWIKI dataset, it reduced the runtime of Qwen2.5-72B from 21 hours to 1 hour, cutting token consumption by over 80% while narrowing performance gaps significantly.

This work pioneers a new direction for applied AI, showing how intelligent prompt design can serve as a powerful, efficient, and interpretable alternative to traditional fine-tuning for specific tasks, optimizing LLM infrastructure costs.
