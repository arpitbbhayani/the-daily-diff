For Emacs users, a truly integrated and powerful terminal experience can significantly boost developer productivity. Enter Ghostel.el, a new terminal emulator powered by libghostty, bringing a host of advanced features directly into your editor.

This is not just another terminal; it offers deep shell integration, intelligent input modes, and features like bookmarking, link detection, and inline images. Imagine seamless context switching without leaving Emacs.

One of its standout capabilities is robust remote terminal support, including automatic shell integration and terminfo setup for remote hosts. This streamlines working with distant servers, making your workflow smoother and more efficient.

If you spend a significant portion of your day in Emacs and the command line, exploring Ghostel.el could unlock new levels of efficiency and a more cohesive development environment.

Enhance your Emacs setup, enhance your productivity.
