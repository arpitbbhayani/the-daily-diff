Maximizing the semantic quality of LLM-generated summaries while minimizing token usage is a critical challenge, and this framework offers a compelling solution. It focuses on opinion summarization while cutting costs.

The approach combines multidimensional classification, like sentiment and topics, with stratified sampling strategies. This selects compact yet highly representative subsets of opinions before they ever reach the LLM.

Tailored prompts then guide the LLM to produce balanced summaries that accurately surface strengths and weaknesses. This significantly reduces token usage and computational cost.

If you are working with LLMs for summarization, especially on large opinionated datasets, this method shows how intelligent data preprocessing can lead to better, cheaper, and more faithful outputs. It is about making your LLMs smarter consumers of data.

Smart sampling makes LLM summarization more efficient and accurate.
