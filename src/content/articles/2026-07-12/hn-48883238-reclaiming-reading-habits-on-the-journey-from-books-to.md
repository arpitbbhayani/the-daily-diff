Many of us struggle with focus in our hyper-connected world, finding deep reading harder than ever. This essay explores one person's journey to reclaim their ability to read extensively.

The author recounts facing constant pressures that cultivated short attention spans and insatiable dopamine cravings, which directly hindered deep engagement with books.

It is a powerful reminder that cultivating focus is a skill. Re-engaging with long-form content is not just about personal enjoyment, but also critical for continuous learning and problem-solving as an engineer.

This introspection offers practical value for improving developer productivity and effective engineering practices.
