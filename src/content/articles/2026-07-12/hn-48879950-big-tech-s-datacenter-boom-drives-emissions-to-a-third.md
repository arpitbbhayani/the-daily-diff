Datacenter growth, fueled by AI demand, has driven big tech's carbon emissions to staggering levels.Microsoft's emissions rose 25% last year alone, and collectively, Microsoft, Amazon, and Google's output reached 119 million metric tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent.That is about one-third of France's total emissions.This raises critical questions for engineers designing scalable systems and AI infrastructure.How can we balance rapid innovation with environmental responsibility?The push for net-zero goals faces immense pressure from the computational demands of AI.Consider the long-term impact in your system design choices.
