Coverage Path Planning (CPP) is more than just vacuum cleaners. It is a fundamental problem in robotics, driving applications from inspection and mapping to agriculture and demining.

This comprehensive survey dives deep into CPP, tracing its evolution from classical methods to modern advancements. It breaks down approaches for single-robot, multi-robot, 3D, and even learning-based systems.

One fascinating area explored is multi-robot CPP, where coordination becomes paramount to efficiently cover complex workspaces. Imagine fleets of drones or autonomous vehicles working together.

The paper also highlights critical open challenges: scalable online planning, robust multi-robot coordination, and integrating learning for platform-constrained and resource-aware coverage.

If you work with robotics or multi-agent systems, this survey is an excellent resource to grasp the state of the art and future directions in path planning.
