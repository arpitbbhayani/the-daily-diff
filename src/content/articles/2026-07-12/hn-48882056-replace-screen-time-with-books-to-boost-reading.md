Reading more books is a powerful lever for career growth and continuous learning, and it is more achievable than you might think. One engineer shares how they shifted from fewer than ten books a year to a book a week.

The core insight is not about "making time" but about "reading every single time you are not doing something else." This means replacing habitual phone-checking during idle moments with picking up a book instead.

Removing distracting social media and streaming apps from your phone creates the necessary void. After a few days, your brain starts remapping, making the book a natural choice during those small windows of inactivity.

Always carrying a book and dedicating short bursts-like upon waking, before sleeping, or while cooking-can transform your reading habit. This simple shift in behavior can unlock significant personal and professional development.

Invest in yourself by investing in your reading habit.
