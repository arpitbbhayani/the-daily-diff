Windows 11 Copilot, designed to tell you what is slowing down your PC, is ironically using up to 1GB of RAM itself. This AI agent, implemented as a web app with a private copy of Edge, creates an interesting performance paradox.It is a stark reminder that even tools built to optimize performance can introduce significant overhead if not carefully engineered. This kind of resource consumption can negate the very benefits it promises, especially on systems with limited memory.For senior engineers, this underscores the importance of efficient design choices and considering the hidden costs of AI integration, even in user-facing features. The trade-offs between functionality, convenience, and system resources are always critical.Understanding these real-world implications of AI agents on system performance is key for robust software architecture.
