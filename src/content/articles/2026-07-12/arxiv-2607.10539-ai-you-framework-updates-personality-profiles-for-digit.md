Building genuinely consistent digital twins requires continuous personality updates and robust, multi-layered memory.

Existing approaches for inferring user traits and generating persona-consistent responses often rely on static prompting, leading to uncertainty and trait drift during long interactions. AI YOU tackles this by continually updating a 22-dimensional personality profile.

This framework combines prompting with Bayesian updating and conformal prediction for persona inference. A periodically refreshed memory anchor and a three-layer cognitive memory preserve consistency over extended interactions.

AI YOU achieves conformal coverage ranging from 0.921 to 0.976 and significantly enhances persona fidelity over 100 turns compared to static prompting. It also reduces trait drift even under adversarial multi-agent settings.

Create dynamic, consistent digital twins that truly remember and evolve.
