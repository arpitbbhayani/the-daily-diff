Have you ever wanted to trace the intellectual lineage of modern tech? 'Billion Dollar PDFs' is a directory of memos, white papers, and essays that literally shaped capital flow and introduced new mental models.It is a goldmine for understanding foundational concepts like the Transformer architecture from "Attention Is All You Need," which powers today's LLMs.You will also find classics like "Why Software Is Eating the World," which remains incredibly relevant.This collection provides invaluable context on how key ideas, from AI breakthroughs to fundamental market shifts, emerged and gained traction.It is an essential resource for any senior engineer looking to deepen their understanding of our industry's strategic underpinnings.Dive in and connect the dots.
