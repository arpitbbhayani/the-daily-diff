Moving files between machines does not have to be a chore involving temporary servers or complicated firewall rules. Croc offers a refreshingly simple command-line solution.

This open-source tool allows any two computers to transfer files and folders securely, using a relay for seamless connectivity across networks. It implements end-to-end encryption with PAKE, ensuring your data remains private.

What makes it stand out? Cross-platform support for Windows, Linux, and Mac, the ability to resume interrupted transfers, and no need for manual port-forwarding. It just works.

For engineers often needing to share binaries, logs, or quick data between development environments, Croc is a game-changer.

Simplify your file transfers, securely and efficiently.
