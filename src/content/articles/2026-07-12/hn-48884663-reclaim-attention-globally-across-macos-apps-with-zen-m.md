Struggling with digital distractions on macOS? "Zen Mode" offers a simple yet powerful solution to reclaim your focus.This Hammerspoon script creates a global focus mode: your current working window takes center stage, stretching to full height, while everything else-Dock, Menu Bar, other windows-fades to black.Unlike app-specific zen modes, this works across *every* Mac application. One hotkey, consistent focus, whether you are coding in your terminal or researching in your browser.It is like a spotlight on your work, without forcing full-screen. This tool is a prime example of effective engineering for developer productivity, minimizing context switching and maximizing deep work.Boost your concentration and workflow with this elegant focus tool for macOS.
