Developing Software Engineering (SE) agents is rapidly changing established engineering practices. This study provides a rare look into how practitioners are actually building these systems.

It highlights a significant shift towards evaluation-driven development. This means evaluation is not just a final step, but a continuous loop steering iteration, with specifications becoming versioned artifacts for both humans and agents.

Practitioners are facing new bottlenecks, including unreliable evaluation signals and 'comprehension debt' as code generation outpaces human understanding. These are real-world problems demanding new solutions.

This paper details a seven-stage workflow and identifies six key challenges, along with practical strategies teams are adopting. If you are building or planning to build SE agents, this offers critical lessons from the front lines on what works and what does not.
