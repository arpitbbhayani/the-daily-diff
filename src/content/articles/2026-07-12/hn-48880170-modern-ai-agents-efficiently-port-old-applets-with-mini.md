The promise of AI coding agents often feels distant, but here is a concrete example of their practical power. Mathematician Terence Tao successfully used modern agents to port two dozen of his old Java 1.0 applets to Javascript in mere hours.

These were not trivial programs; some dated back to 1999, visualizing complex mathematical objects like honeycombs and Besicovitch sets. Historically, such porting efforts were incredibly time-consuming and prone to error.

What is truly remarkable is the agent's performance: only one minor bug was found in the ported code, and the agent even identified two bugs in the original Java code. This goes beyond simple translation; it implies a level of understanding and debugging.

This showcases how AI agents can dramatically enhance developer productivity, offering a powerful tool for modernizing legacy systems and breathing new life into old projects.

Embrace AI agents for more than just new code, they can revive your past.
