Large language model agents are dramatically accelerating the inverse design of complex materials like metal-organic frameworks.

Designing Metal-Organic Frameworks (MOFs) for gas separation is challenging due to a vast design space and multiple simultaneous constraints. The LEMO Agent framework uses LLM agents for closed-loop inverse design in this domain.

LEMO Agent couples language-based candidate generation with MOFid standardization, explicit validity checking, Transformer-based property prediction, and structured design memory. It iterates through generate, validate, evaluate, and remember cycles.

Evaluated on CH4/N2 and CO2/N2 separation, LEMO Agent enriches high-performing candidates, improves predicted separation performance, and maintains broad chemical diversity. This showcases agents as scalable design engines for scientific discovery.

Agents are not just for code, they are designing tomorrow's advanced materials.
