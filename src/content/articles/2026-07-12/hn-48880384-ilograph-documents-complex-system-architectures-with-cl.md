How do you truly understand a complex distributed system? Visual documentation, like sequence diagrams, is absolutely essential. This resource provides examples illustrating serverless back-ends, distributed load testing systems on AWS, and even Stack Overflow's physical datacenter networking.

Good diagrams are not just pretty pictures; they are critical tools for onboarding new engineers and ensuring everyone understands data flows and interdependencies.

Invest in clear visual documentation for your systems; it pays dividends in clarity and reduced cognitive load.
