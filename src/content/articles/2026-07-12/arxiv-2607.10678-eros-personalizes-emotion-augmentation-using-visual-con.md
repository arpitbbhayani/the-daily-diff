Imagine an AI that not only generates realistic content but also understands and personalizes human emotional responses. This is the promise of EROS, a new hybrid AI framework.

EROS (Emotion-augmented geneRatiOn System) integrates symbolic reasoning with deep learning. It discovers generalizable affective rules from image-emotion datasets and predicts visual modifications that steer emotional responses toward desired targets.

What is truly innovative is its personalized approach. EROS uses an expandable memory bank for inference-time personalization, adapting to new users and their unique affective preferences *without model fine-tuning*.

Human psychophysics experiments show EROS elicits target emotional responses more effectively than state-of-the-art large multimodal models. This has profound implications for mental health applications, adaptive media, and human-computer interaction.

AI that understands your feelings is no longer science fiction.
