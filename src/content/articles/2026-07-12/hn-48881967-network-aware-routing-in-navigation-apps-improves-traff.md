Traffic congestion wastes an average of 2.6 years of a driver's life and accounts for 10% of global CO2 emissions. Google Research is tackling this with network-aware routing.

Their approach, detailed in "Urban congestion relief experiments through routing-app interventions," demonstrates how navigation apps can optimize traffic flow system-wide, not just for individual vehicles.

This builds on their Project Green Light, which uses AI to optimize city traffic lights. The work involves complex system design and large-scale empirical validation, a true applied AI challenge.

It shows the power of AI and collaboration to create scalable systems that impact real-world distributed problems.
