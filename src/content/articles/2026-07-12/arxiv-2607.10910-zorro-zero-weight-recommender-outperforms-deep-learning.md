Imagine a personalized recommender system that requires no training and operates 600 times faster than state-of-the-art deep learning models, yet performs nearly on par in online A/B tests. ZoRRO (Zero-Weight Personalized Recommender System) delivers exactly that for scalable news recommendation.

This framework is entirely training-free and designed for real-world deployment. Its remarkable speed and efficiency challenge the notion that complex, heavy models are always necessary for high-performance recommendation. ZoRRO highlights a gap between offline and online performance metrics, showing how models with similar click-through rates can produce vastly different news flows.

ZoRRO is a testament to ingenious system design in applied AI, offering a pragmatic and highly efficient solution for large-scale personalization without the heavy computational burden of traditional neural networks.
