AI 2040: Plan A proposes a radical vision to prevent AI-driven catastrophe. It suggests delaying superintelligence until 2040, demanding total research transparency, and encouraging dozens of global companies to reach the frontier simultaneously under 'mutually assured compute destruction.'This is a bold framework, not a prediction, to stress-test policy recommendations for managing advanced AI. It forces us to confront the ethical and strategic challenges of a rapidly evolving technological landscape.For any senior engineer invested in the future of AI, understanding these high-level policy discussions is paramount. It shapes the environment in which we will build and deploy the next generation of intelligent systems.
