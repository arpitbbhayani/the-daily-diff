Reasoning in large models often relies on Chain-of-Thought. What if reasoning could unfold through *temporally connected frames*?

OpenCoF introduces "Chain-of-Frame" (CoF) reasoning, a novel approach enabled by video generation models. Researchers built OpenCoF-17K, a reasoning video dataset, and Wan-CoF, a fine-tuned model to study CoF behavior.

Wan-CoF achieved significant gains over baselines, showing that diverse temporal supervision and explicit visual/textual reasoning tokens dramatically improve video reasoning capabilities.

Enhance your LLM reasoning by teaching it to think in frames, not just words.
