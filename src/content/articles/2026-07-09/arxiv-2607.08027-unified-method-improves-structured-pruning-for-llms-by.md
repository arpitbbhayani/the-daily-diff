Deploying large language models efficiently is a major infrastructure challenge. This paper presents an improved structured pruning method for LLMs that achieves practical inference speedup while maintaining accuracy.

The technique combines power transformation, sign-preserving score aggregation, and percentile-based outlier removal to adapt unstructured pruning methods for structured pruning. This leads to comparable accuracy to unstructured pruning but with tangible performance gains.

For engineers working on LLM infrastructure, optimizing model size and inference speed is critical. This research provides concrete methods to make LLMs more performant and cost-effective, directly impacting deployment strategies and operational costs.
