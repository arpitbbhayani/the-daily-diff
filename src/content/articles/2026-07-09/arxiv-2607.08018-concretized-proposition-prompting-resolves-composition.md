LLMs often struggle with the "Composition-Knowledge Dichotomy," balancing complex reasoning with accurate factual recall. Concretized Proposition Prompting (CPP) offers a solution by explicitly concretizing relevant propositions within prompts.

This framework significantly boosts reasoning performance, especially in knowledge-intensive domains like medical benchmarks, while remaining competitive in math. CPP is shown to be scalable across various foundation models.

For engineers engaged in prompt engineering and applied AI, CPP provides a fundamental paradigm shift. It is about bridging the gap between how LLMs compose logic and retrieve knowledge, leading to more robust and factually grounded outputs.
