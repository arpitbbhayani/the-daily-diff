Fluid Computing aims to spread applications across cloud, edge, and devices, but how do you offload tasks efficiently *and* privately in dynamic, device-to-device networks?

This paper introduces a coded task offloading scheme that combines linear secret sharing with traditional offloading. Tasks are encoded into redundant shares, enabling threshold-based recovery, straggler mitigation, and strong privacy preservation.

The framework jointly optimizes for delay and energy while penalizing theoretical privacy leakage. Experiments show coded offloading improves delay-energy trade-offs over classical schemes, revealing an inherent delay-energy-privacy trade-off.

Achieve optimal performance in fluid computing while guaranteeing privacy, even in adversarial execution settings.
