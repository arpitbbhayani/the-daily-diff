Leveraging LLMs for complex document analysis is a mainstream task, but flattening documents into plain text loses critical hierarchical structure. DocMaster addresses this with a system that preserves document layouts.

It parses documents into hierarchical trees and builds a structure-aware semantic index. This enables significantly more accurate document filtering and in-depth question answering, a massive upgrade for RAG systems.

For engineers building knowledge retrieval or automated analysis systems with LLMs, DocMaster provides a concrete architectural pattern to enhance reasoning. It is a vital step toward making LLMs truly effective with long, structured documents.
