DeepSeek is making a bold move, pivoting to design and produce its own AI chips. This signals a critical trend in the AI industry: vertical integration of hardware and software for optimal performance.This strategic shift is driven by the immense compute demands of large language models and the desire for greater control over the performance-efficiency frontier. Owning the silicon becomes a competitive advantage for LLM infrastructure.For senior software engineers, this means deeper consideration of hardware-software co-design in applied AI. The future of AI is not just about models, but the underlying infrastructure that powers them.
