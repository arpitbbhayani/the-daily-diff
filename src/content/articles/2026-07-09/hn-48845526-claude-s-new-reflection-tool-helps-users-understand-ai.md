Are you really maximizing your LLM usage? Anthropic's new reflection feature for Claude lets you track and visualize your interaction patterns.

This beta tool helps you understand when and how you use AI most effectively, covering key topics and task types over various timeframes. It also prompts critical self-reflection, asking questions like "What is one thing you want to keep doing yourself, even if Claude could do it faster?"

You can also set quiet hours or schedule breaks, aligning your AI use with personal goals. This is about building "AI fluency" - delegating tasks, describing goals effectively, detecting AI errors, and deciding when to rely on AI.

Mastering your AI interaction can unlock significant productivity gains and refine your problem-solving approach.
