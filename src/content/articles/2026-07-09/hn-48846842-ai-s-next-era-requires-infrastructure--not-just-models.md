The conversation around AI is shifting: the next era is not just about models, but the infrastructure that makes them reliable, auditable, and cost-effective in production. This article highlights why this is critical for enterprise adoption.The author states that annual AI budgets are being blown away in months, forcing a focus on system and data ownership. This is a clear call to action for senior engineers to prioritize robust LLM infrastructure and scalable system design.It is about moving beyond experimentation to delivering measurable ROI. Understanding these infrastructure imperatives is key to successfully integrating applied AI into any large organization.
