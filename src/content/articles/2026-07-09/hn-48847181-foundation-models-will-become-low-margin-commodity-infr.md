LLM token pricing is volatile, but the market is heading towards an equilibrium. Expect significant changes as a trillion dollars or more in data center capital expenditure comes online.

Inference efficiency is improving rapidly, and new models vary wildly in token use. The current capacity crunch, primarily driven by software development use cases, is just a fraction of potential consumer demand, which today's infrastructure cannot yet support.

Currently, inference boasts 40-50 percent gross margins. However, the long-term outlook suggests foundation models may become low-margin commodity infrastructure. The critical question is whether they retain pricing power or become utility providers.

Understanding these underlying economics is essential for anyone building or investing in AI solutions.
