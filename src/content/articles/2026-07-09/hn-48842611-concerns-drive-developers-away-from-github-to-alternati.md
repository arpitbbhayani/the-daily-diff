Are developers truly ditching GitHub? This article explores the growing trend of moving to Codeberg and self-hosting alternatives, driven by concerns over technical stability and Microsoft's ownership.It highlights a crucial discussion about platform dependency versus control and sovereignty over your code. The reasons are not trivial, touching upon system reliability and community values.This shift impacts engineering practices, encouraging a re-evaluation of centralized services versus distributed, self-managed solutions. It is a vital conversation for any engineer considering the long-term implications of their toolchain choices.
