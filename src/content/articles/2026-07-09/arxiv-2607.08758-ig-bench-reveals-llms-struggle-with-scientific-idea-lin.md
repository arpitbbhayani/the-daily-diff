How do you benchmark an AI's ability to truly innovate, not just regurgitate? Scientific ideas rarely start from a blank page; they evolve.

IdeaGene-Bench introduces a unique approach to evaluating LLM-based systems on "scientific lineage reasoning" and "lineage-grounded idea generation." It uses an "Idea Genome" framework to track inheritance, mutation, and recombination of concepts.

This benchmark includes 1,961 golden lineage traces and 1,085 curated Idea Genome objects. Initial experiments show that even the strongest systems achieve only 27.3% exact accuracy on lineage reasoning.

Challenge your LLM agents with the true complexity of scientific thought: the evolution of ideas.
