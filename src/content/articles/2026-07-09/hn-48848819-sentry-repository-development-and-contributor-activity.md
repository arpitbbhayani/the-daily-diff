Managing a large GitHub organization? You need Devthropology. This tool goes beyond basic analytics, offering deep insights into repository activity.

It tracks contributor activity, showing new and churned developers, median author age, and time-to-merge statistics. For instance, it can reveal a 175 percent increase in time-to-merge over 30 days, signaling potential bottlenecks.

Understanding these metrics is crucial for engineering leadership. You can identify patterns in code changes, files changed, and author tenure, all vital for assessing project health and team dynamics.

This provides a concrete way to measure and improve engineering practices, ensuring your development efforts remain efficient and sustainable.
