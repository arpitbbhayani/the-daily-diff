The explosive growth of AI is fueling a data center boom, but the environmental costs are staggering. Texas is becoming a major hub, with hundreds of facilities like OpenAI's Stargate under development.

Many of these data centers are constructing massive, natural-gas-powered electrical plants, often with little public oversight. Residents are experiencing direct impacts, from dust clouds to air pollution, due to regulatory loopholes.

This raises critical questions about the sustainability and responsible deployment of large-scale LLM infrastructure. As engineers, we must consider the broader implications of the systems we build, including their energy consumption and environmental footprint.

The industry needs to confront these challenges now to ensure future growth does not come at an irreparable cost.
