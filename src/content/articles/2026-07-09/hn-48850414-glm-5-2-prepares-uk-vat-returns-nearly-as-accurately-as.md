GLM 5.2, an open-weights AI model, is nearly as accurate as a human book-keeper for VAT returns, but at less than 1% of the cost. This is a game-changer for automating compliance.The model processed 59 transactions in 68 minutes, achieving a net position off by only 7 pence. This demonstrates powerful potential for applied AI in highly structured, detail-oriented tasks.It is a compelling case for leveraging LLM reasoning to drive efficiency in business operations. Imagine the impact across various industries. This is not just a theoretical improvement; it is a measurable, cost-saving reality.
