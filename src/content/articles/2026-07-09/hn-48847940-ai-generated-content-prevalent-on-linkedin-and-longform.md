AI-generated content is everywhere, and LinkedIn is particularly saturated. A new report finds that one in four longform posts are fully AI-generated, blurring the lines of authentic communication.This data, collected via a Chrome extension, highlights a significant challenge for developer productivity: how do we filter out the 'AI slop' to find genuine insights and credible information?The proliferation of AI content demands sharper critical evaluation skills from every professional. It is time to refine your information diet and ensure you are consuming genuine, human-generated expertise.
