Ever wonder what an AI is "thinking" before it generates a response? Lucid, a new web tool, provides a real-time window into a language model's internal representations.

Using a "Jacobian lens," Lucid lets you see the concepts an LLM holds, layer by layer, between your input and its output. This is a powerful way to debug and understand the model's reasoning process.

You can observe which concepts enter the "workspace," how they evolve, and even identify those the model considers but ultimately does not articulate. This level of transparency is invaluable for anyone working with LLMs.

This tool offers a profound insight into the mechanics of artificial intelligence, allowing for deeper exploration of LLM reasoning.
