The internet often demonizes subscriptions, but this perspective argues they are the best model for both indie developers and users in the mobile app ecosystem.

Subscriptions create aligned interests. Users pay for value received, motivating developers to consistently maintain and improve their applications. If the value diminishes, users stop paying - a fair deal for everyone involved.

Consider the alternative: one-time purchases often mean stagnant apps, or worse, abandoned projects once initial sales dry up. Subscriptions ensure that the underlying infrastructure is funded and ongoing development can continue.

It is a sustainable path for delivering evolving, high-quality software, contrasting sharply with models that encourage rapid feature additions without long-term commitment.
