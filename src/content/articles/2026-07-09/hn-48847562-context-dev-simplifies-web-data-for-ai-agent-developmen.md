Context.dev is launching an API that directly addresses a critical need for AI agents: obtaining live, structured data from any website. This is a game-changer for RAG systems and agentic workflows.It tackles the pain of building and maintaining complex scraping infrastructure, allowing teams to focus on core product features. Imagine your AI agents pulling real-time, high-quality context on demand.This platform simplifies accessing web, brand, and company data, replacing multiple vendors with a single, efficient solution. It is a powerful boost for applied AI development, enabling more informed and effective agents.
