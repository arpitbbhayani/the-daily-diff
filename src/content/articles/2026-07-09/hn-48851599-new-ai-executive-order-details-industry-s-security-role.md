A new US Executive Order on AI is reshaping how government agencies approach secure software delivery. The directive primarily places the burden of securing "frontier models" on the developers themselves, not government users.

However, agencies are mandated to leverage AI systems to enhance their own cyber defenses, accelerating adoption of AI-enabled defensive tools. This signals a dual focus: securing the AI, and using AI for security.

For senior engineers, this means a growing demand for expertise in both robust AI development and the application of AI to secure complex systems, especially with continuous authorization becoming a key theme. This shift could drive significant innovation in AI governance and defense.
