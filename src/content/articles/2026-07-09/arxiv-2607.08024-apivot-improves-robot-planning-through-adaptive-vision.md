Long-horizon robot planning demands joint reasoning across semantic task structure and geometric feasibility. APIVOT, a new VLM-based planner, tackles this by adaptively interleaving language and visual "thoughts."

APIVOT uses language for high-level semantic reasoning, while visual thoughts act as imagined future states, internally verifying geometric feasibility. This approach significantly outperforms prior planning frameworks, especially in complex, spatially constrained kitchen tasks.

For engineers building AI agents for robotics, this paper presents a compelling model for creating more capable and robust planners. It is about enabling robots to not just understand commands but to also "visualize" and validate their actions in real-world environments.
