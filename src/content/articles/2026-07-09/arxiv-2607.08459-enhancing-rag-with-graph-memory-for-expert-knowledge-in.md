Conversational retrieval with RAG is powerful for extractive tasks, but often struggles with holistic collection interpretation and dynamic integration of expert knowledge. This is a critical limitation for complex domains.

This paper introduces a document analysis system for historical digital libraries that supports "on-the-fly knowledge modeling." It stores facts from archivists and document retrieval in a graph-based structure.

This graph acts as a memory for the language model, enabling increasingly complex queries that involve long-term dependencies across documents, link discovery, and rich expert knowledge not explicitly in source texts.

Enhance your RAG systems by moving beyond simple extraction to dynamic, graph-based knowledge modeling for deeper insights.
