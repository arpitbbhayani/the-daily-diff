Are your Vision-Language Models truly ready for autonomous driving, or do they just recognize objects?

AUTOPILOT-VQA introduces a critical benchmark for incident-centric dashcam video understanding. It challenges models with structured questions around real-world driving incidents, evaluating reasoning on weather, traffic, road conditions, and accident avoidability.

This goes beyond basic scene understanding, demanding temporally grounded, safety-aware reasoning. If you are building AI for safety-critical applications, this dataset helps move from theoretical performance to real-world reliability.

Benchmarking for genuine reasoning is essential when lives are on the line.
