Large language models often disproportionately reflect Western values, limiting their global applicability. PLURAL introduces a large-scale dataset for value alignment, grounded in the Integrated Values Survey across 92 countries.

This dataset transforms survey responses into ~500,000 synthetic preference triplets, enabling LLMs to better reflect diverse cultural profiles. Training on PLURAL reduced mean absolute error by up to 27.7% in alignment with target countries.

For engineers building global applied AI products, ensuring cultural value alignment is paramount. PLURAL provides a scalable resource to move beyond biased models, fostering more equitable and universally relevant LLM outputs.
