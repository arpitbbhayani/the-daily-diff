Research software collaborations often suffer from fragmented decision-making, with rationale lost across chats, meetings, and pull requests. Aleena, an open-source "alignment agent," directly addresses this challenge.

Aleena uses GitHub as a central hub, transforming multi-modal stakeholder interactions into structured project records. It surfaces risks, tracks open questions, and crucially, preserves decision continuity throughout the software development lifecycle.

For engineers managing complex projects, particularly in research or highly collaborative environments, Aleena exemplifies how AI agents can boost developer productivity and maintain shared understanding without replacing human decision-making. It is about intelligent process augmentation.
