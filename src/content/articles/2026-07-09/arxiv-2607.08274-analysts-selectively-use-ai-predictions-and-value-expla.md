Deploying AI in high-stakes environments like crime linkage analysis reveals critical lessons about human-AI trust and interaction.

This industrial study of an AI-enabled decision-support tool showed that analysts used AI predictions selectively, frequently validating them against behavioral evidence. This reflects a healthy "partial trust" and continued reliance on established practices.

Analysts valued the presented model features as explanations, but identified opportunities to improve their presentation and integration into the workflow. This highlights the need for AI to augment, not replace, expert judgment.

For usable and trustworthy AI, explanations must be integrated seamlessly, and validation pathways must be clear.
