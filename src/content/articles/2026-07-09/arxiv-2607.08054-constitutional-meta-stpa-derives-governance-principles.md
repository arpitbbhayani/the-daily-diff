When LLMs draft safety analysis artifacts, who validates the LLM itself? This paper tackles this critical question with Constitutional Meta-STPA, a framework for self-validating LLM-assisted safety tools.

By applying Systems-Theoretic Process Analysis (STPA) to the AI tool, it derives a governance constitution of 29 principles, each bound to a code enforcement point. This ensures auditability and mitigates risks like hallucinated standards.

For senior engineers developing or relying on AI-assisted engineering tools, this research offers a robust blueprint for building trust. It is about ensuring that the tools designed to ensure safety are themselves rigorously safe and verifiable.
