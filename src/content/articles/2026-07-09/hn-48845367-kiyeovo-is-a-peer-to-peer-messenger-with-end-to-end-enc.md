Decentralized communication is making strides with Kiyeovo 1.0, an E2EE P2P messenger just released. This is not just another messaging app; it operates entirely without central servers or user accounts.

Kiyeovo utilizes a Distributed Hash Table (DHT) for peer discovery, allowing direct communication between users. It even includes an optional Tor mode for enhanced anonymity, pushing the boundaries of truly resilient, censor-resistant communication.

For system design enthusiasts, this open-source project showcases practical implementations of P2P architectures and end-to-end encryption, offering valuable insights into building robust, privacy-focused distributed applications that minimize reliance on centralized infrastructure.
