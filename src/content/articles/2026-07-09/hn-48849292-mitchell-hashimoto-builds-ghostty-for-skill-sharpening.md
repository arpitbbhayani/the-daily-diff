Mitchell Hashimoto, the mind behind Terraform and Vault, shares his journey back to deep systems programming with Zig. He is building Ghostty, a terminal emulator, to sharpen skills dulled by years in distributed systems.This interview is a goldmine for understanding what drives senior engineers. It is about the continuous pursuit of technical mastery and the joy of working on low-level challenges like GPU programming and cache locality.It is a powerful reminder that even after building massive companies, the desire to learn and build fundamental software remains strong. This is effective engineering in action.
