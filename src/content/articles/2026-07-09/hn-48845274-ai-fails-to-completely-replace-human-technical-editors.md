LLMs are not replacing human technical editors anytime soon. A recent experiment tested Claude Opus 4.8 and Fable on an article seeded with 23 errors, finding LLMs struggled with logical inconsistencies and nuanced context.

While these models were decent at catching basic grammar and homophone errors, they often introduced new mistakes or missed complex structural issues that a human editor would easily spot. For example, they failed to identify when the article's own framework was defined backwards.

This highlights a critical point for engineers using applied AI: LLMs are powerful tools for augmentation, but they currently lack the deep understanding and critical reasoning needed for truly complex tasks. Always be aware of where human oversight remains indispensable.
