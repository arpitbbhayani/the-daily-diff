Deploying an AI assistant? Understanding real-world usage at scale is critical, especially when self-reported data can be misleading.

This study provides a massive descriptive analysis of Syntea, an AI-based learning assistant used by 77,543 students. Researchers used objective log data to examine usage patterns across gender, age, study cluster, and degree.

The findings show that Syntea is embedded in many learners' routines, but usage differs significantly across demographic and structural contexts. This empirical foundation is invaluable for the informed development of educational AI.

Do not guess at user behavior. Ground your AI development in large-scale, objective usage data.
