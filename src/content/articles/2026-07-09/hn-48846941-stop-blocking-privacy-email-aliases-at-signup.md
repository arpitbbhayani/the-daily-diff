Your signup flow might be blocking legitimate users. One engineer found their own privacy alias blocked by a burner email list they previously built, leading to a crucial re-evaluation.

The core distinction is between public shared inboxes and personal aliases like those from Proton Mail, Firefox Relay, or Apple Hide My Email. Blocking all "burner" domains indiscriminately alienates users protecting their privacy, not just spammers.

Spammers have already moved past these simple domain checks. Your policies should too. This shift improves user experience and ensures your system remains effective against actual threats, rather than penalizing privacy-conscious individuals.

It is time to adapt our engineering practices to today's user privacy landscape.
