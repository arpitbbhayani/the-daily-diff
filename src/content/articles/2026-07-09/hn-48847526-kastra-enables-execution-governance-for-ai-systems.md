As AI coding agents become more powerful, ensuring their actions align with organizational policies is critical. Kastra.ai addresses this with a new policy enforcement tool for agents like Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex.

This tool allows engineers to define and enforce guardrails around what an AI agent can and cannot do, moving beyond simple prompts to structured governance. It is a crucial step for integrating AI agents safely into enterprise development workflows.

For senior engineers, implementing such policy enforcement is key to mitigating risks and building trust in automated coding processes. It helps maintain control and auditability as AI systems take on more complex tasks.
