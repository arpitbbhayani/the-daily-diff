Why Lisp still matters in a world dominated by newer languages. This article champions Lisp, not just as a historical curiosity, but as a language that can unlock powerful new ways of thinking for software engineers.Many programmers struggle with Lisp's unconventional syntax, but its features enable capabilities not possible in other languages. It can fundamentally shift your approach to algorithms and system construction.This is not just about adding another language to your resume; it is about expanding your mental models for problem-solving. It is a path to deeper understanding and potentially greater developer productivity.
