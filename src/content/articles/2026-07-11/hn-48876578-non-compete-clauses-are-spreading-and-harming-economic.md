Non-compete clauses are far more common than most engineers realize, and they are not just for executives with trade secrets. Many workers, even those in non-managerial roles, are bound by them.

New OECD research shows between one-fifth and one-third of private-sector workers across 15 countries report being under a non-compete. This includes a significant portion of technical professionals.

These clauses actively suppress wages, reduce job mobility, and hinder overall economic dynamism and innovation. They limit where you can work and for whom, even after leaving a company.

For senior engineers looking to switch roles, start a company, or even just seek better compensation, understanding the fine print of non-competes is crucial. It can dictate your next career move.

Always know what follows you out the door when you leave a job.
