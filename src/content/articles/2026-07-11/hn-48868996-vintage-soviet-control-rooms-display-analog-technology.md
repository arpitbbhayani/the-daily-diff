Step into the past and explore the unique system design of Soviet control rooms. Before CRTs and touchscreens, these massive interfaces relied on hundreds of analog dials, physical switches, and indicator lights. They represent an entirely different paradigm of monitoring and managing complex industrial systems. For engineers focused on scalable and robust systems, observing these pre-digital designs can spark interesting reflections on interface simplicity, physical feedback, and operational resilience under different technological constraints.
