The FCC just approved a "sunlight on demand" space mirror, Earendil-1, as a test for a planned constellation of 50,000 satellites by 2035. This represents an unprecedented scale in distributed space systems. Imagine the system design challenges: managing orbital mechanics for thousands of reflective surfaces, ensuring precise targeting for "sunlight" delivery, and mitigating light pollution. This project, while controversial, offers a fascinating case study in extreme scalable systems and their potential cascading impacts on Earth systems.
