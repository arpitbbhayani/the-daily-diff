A new JavaScript runtime, Ant, is here, aiming to redefine development efficiency. It promises a lightweight core, moving away from the often heavy existing runtimes. Early benchmarks suggest significant startup time improvements, potentially shaving off hundreds of milliseconds from common tasks. This could mean faster CI/CD pipelines and quicker local development loops. For engineers optimizing build times and resource usage, this is a compelling new tool to explore.
