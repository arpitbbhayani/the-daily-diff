Orbit brings real-time satellite tracking to your iPhone, allowing you to visualize over 15,000 orbital objects with augmented reality. Think about the system design behind this: ingesting and processing vast amounts of dynamic orbital data, performing complex celestial mechanics calculations on-device, and rendering it seamlessly in real time. This is not just a cool AR app; it is a solid example of applied data systems and efficient mobile computing. It even incorporates an optional AI chatbot for interactive queries, making it a compelling piece of applied AI and real-time system architecture.
