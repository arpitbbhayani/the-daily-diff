What if a font could be read by humans but baffle AI? Ghost Font is exactly that - an anti-AI font using motion, video, noise, and decoys. Even advanced AI models like Claude Fable and GPT Sol 5.6 Ultra struggle to decipher messages written with it. This is not about complex cryptography, but about exploiting the fundamental differences in human visual processing versus how current AI models perceive text. It highlights that AI's 'understanding' of images is far from human-like, especially when dynamic elements are introduced. This experiment offers fascinating insights into the limitations of applied AI and inspires thought on novel adversarial methods. Sometimes, the simplest tricks can expose the deepest technological gaps.
