The "hard takeoff" AI singularity narrative completely misses the mark when confronted with real-world engineering. Shipping a complex hardware product like a cell phone, or even dealing with basic supply chain issues, reveals the finicky details that even a "superintelligent ChatGPT" would struggle with.

True intelligence in AI development is not just about token quality; it is about grappling with the physical world's constraints. You cannot simply generate a datacenter in the ocean; you must contend with manufacturing errors, component failures, and even barnacles.

These practical challenges underscore that AI, while transformative, operates within the same physical laws and ecological limits as any other technology. There are no magic tricks to circumvent reality.

Yet, a grounded perspective on AI emphasizes the formidable engineering effort required.
