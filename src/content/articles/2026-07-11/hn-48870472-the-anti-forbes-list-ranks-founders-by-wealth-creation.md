Do you only measure wealth by what someone *keeps*? This "Anti-Forbes List" flips the script, ranking founders by the wealth they *created for others*. It is a fascinating re-evaluation of impact. Some top tech founders, like Jensen Huang of NVIDIA, created trillions in value for shareholders, dwarfing their personal holdings. This shift in perspective makes you consider: what truly defines impact in the tech industry? It is a fresh take on measuring success.
