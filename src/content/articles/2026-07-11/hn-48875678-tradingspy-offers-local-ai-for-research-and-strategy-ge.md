Building robust AI agents requires careful design, especially in sensitive domains like finance. TradingSpy is an open-source, local-first AI trading assistant demonstrating powerful loop engineering.

This agent does not just offer market heatmaps and news catalysts; it generates trading strategies and performs backtesting against historical data. All of this happens locally, ensuring complete privacy.

The project highlights how to combine various AI techniques and data sources into a cohesive system for actionable insights without external data sharing. It provides a blueprint for secure, private AI applications.

For engineers interested in agentic AI and applied AI systems, this is a fantastic case study. You can explore how a multi-agent approach can handle complex tasks from research to strategy validation.
