The rise of AI voice cloning is creating an alarming new reality: a Chinese voice actor is now forced to prove he is human because AI copies of his voice are so prevalent.

Platforms are flagging his authentic recordings as synthetic, leading to lost income and a battle for his professional identity. He hears his voice narrating content he never created, from movie explainers to conspiracy theories.

This scenario exposes a critical challenge for applied AI: the ease of replication versus the rights of creators. It is not just about technology; it is about identity, ownership, and livelihood.

As engineers, we must consider the profound ethical implications of our AI tools and work towards safeguards that protect human creativity.

When AI becomes indistinguishable, who proves originality?
