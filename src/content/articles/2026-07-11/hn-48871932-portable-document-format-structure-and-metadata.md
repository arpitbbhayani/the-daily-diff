Imagine anticipating the future of AI in 1965. I.J. Good did exactly that, laying the groundwork for what we now discuss as superintelligence.

His paper, "Speculations Concerning the First Ultraintelligent Machine," proposed a machine capable of surpassing all human intellectual activities. It would even design better machines.

This recursive self-improvement idea is core to modern AI theory, predicting the exponential growth we are now starting to witness. It is not just a historical curiosity; it is a roadmap.

Understanding these early insights helps ground our current discussions on advanced AI and its profound implications.

The seeds of today's AI were planted decades ago.
