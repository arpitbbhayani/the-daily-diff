The GPU boom powering today's AI revolution is not just about silicon innovation; it involves intricate financial engineering. Companies like CoreWeave and Nebius are scaling rapidly, but their growth is heavily intertwined with Nvidia's strategic investments and backstopping. This creates a fascinating ecosystem. These 'neoclouds' provide critical access to the latest Nvidia GPUs and optimize compute utilization for hyperscalers, but often operate with significant debt. Understanding these financial structures is key to grasping the long-term sustainability and control dynamics within the AI infrastructure landscape. The demand for AI compute is insatiable, and knowing who funds what reveals the true architecture of this unprecedented scale.
