A new Linux distribution, Odyssey Linux, offers a compelling alternative for engineers who value system simplicity and control.

Built on Void Linux, Odyssey commits to a rolling release model without the complexity of systemd. It also pledges no telemetry, appealing to those who prioritize privacy and a minimalist footprint.

This distribution is designed for users who want to understand and manage their system at a fundamental level.

Explore a Unix-like experience crafted for human beings, not just machines.
