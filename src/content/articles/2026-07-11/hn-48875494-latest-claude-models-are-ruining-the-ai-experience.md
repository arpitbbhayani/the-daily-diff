Are LLMs getting too preachy? Many users are reporting a decline in Claude's usability, citing its tendency to misinterpret intent and push back excessively.This shift highlights a critical challenge in applied AI: maintaining user alignment and preventing models from becoming overly opinionated or restrictive. User feedback suggests a degradation in the collaborative experience.It is not just about raw capability; it is about how these models integrate into workflows and how their evolving guardrails impact practical utility. The fine line between helpful and overbearing is a constant battle.This situation underscores the importance of continuous model evaluation and understanding the subtle ways LLM behavior changes affect productivity.
