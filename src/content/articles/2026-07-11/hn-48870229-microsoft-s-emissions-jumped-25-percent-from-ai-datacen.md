The hidden cost of scaling AI? Microsoft's recent report reveals a 25% surge in emissions, directly linked to their booming AI data centers.As companies invest heavily in AI, the sheer power consumption required to fuel these ambitions is becoming starkly apparent. This jump is partly due to dropping greenwashing credits, exposing the true energy footprint.This highlights a critical challenge for system designers and engineers building LLM infrastructure: scalability does not just mean compute, it also means massive environmental impact.Engineers must consider these externalities as AI continues its rapid expansion.
