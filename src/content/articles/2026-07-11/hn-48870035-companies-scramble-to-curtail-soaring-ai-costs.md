AI is powerful, but its operational costs are quickly becoming a major hurdle for companies. Many enterprises are scrambling to find ways to reduce the soaring expenses associated with deploying and scaling AI models.

This is not just about compute. It involves substantial infrastructure, specialized talent, and continuous optimization to keep the models running efficiently without breaking the bank.

Imagine a scenario where a successful AI application could bankrupt a company purely due to its inference costs. This highlights the need for innovative approaches to LLM infrastructure and cost-effective system design.

The focus is shifting from simply building AI to building sustainable and economically viable AI solutions. Engineers solving these challenges are delivering immense value.

The path to profitable AI requires deep optimization across the stack.
