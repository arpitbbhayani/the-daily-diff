The Free Software Foundation has been a cornerstone of our industry for decades, championing user freedom through software. Their core message is simple: software should grant users the liberty to run, study, modify, and share it, not just a price point.

This philosophy underpins much of the open-source movement that powers modern infrastructure and development. It is a reminder that the tools we build and use have profound implications beyond their immediate utility.

For engineers, embracing free software principles can lead to more transparent, collaborative, and adaptable systems. It encourages a mindset of shared ownership and continuous improvement within the engineering community.

Freedom in software empowers everyone.
