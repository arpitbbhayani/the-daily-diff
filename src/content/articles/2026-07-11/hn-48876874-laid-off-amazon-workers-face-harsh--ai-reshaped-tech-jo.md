The current tech job market is undergoing a seismic shift, with Amazon's recent layoffs pushing thousands of engineers into a highly competitive landscape. Many are discovering that the roles they once held are simply disappearing, replaced by a scramble for fewer opportunities.

The saturation from widespread cuts across major tech companies like Meta, Microsoft, and Oracle means that securing a new position is no longer a straightforward process. Engineers are facing prolonged job searches and intense competition for every opening.

This environment highlights a critical lesson: career stability is paramount. While rapid growth is appealing, the reality of swift job eliminations underscores the importance of a robust career strategy beyond single employers.

Understanding this dynamic is crucial for any engineer navigating their professional path. It is a stark reminder to continuously adapt and strategize in an unpredictable industry.
