The future of AI is not a given; it is being shaped right now, with two starkly different paths emerging. One vision sees a "technical clergy" managing powerful frontier AI systems, leading to potential mass unemployment but also promises of abundance. The other places humans at the center, using AI as an amplifier for their capabilities.

This article provokes deep questions for every engineer building AI systems today. Are we inadvertently creating a centralized, deity-like AI, or are we empowering a future where AI amplifies human potential broadly?

Engineers are on the front lines of this future. Understanding these divergent paths is essential for navigating the ethical and societal responsibilities inherent in developing AI agents.

Consider the long-term impact of the systems you build.
