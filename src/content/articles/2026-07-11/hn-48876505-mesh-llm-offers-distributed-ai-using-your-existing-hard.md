Running large language models often means hefty bills and surrendering control to API providers. Mesh LLM, built on iroh, offers a compelling alternative: distributed AI computing. This system pools your existing GPUs and memory across multiple machines, presenting them as one cohesive OpenAI-compatible API. Imagine running bigger models without buying bigger GPUs, sharing compute privately within your team. Requests can run locally, route to a peer, or even split across several machines, all managed seamlessly by the mesh. It is about taking back control over where your data goes, what hardware runs your workloads, and fundamentally changing the economics of AI deployment. Centralized AI has its place, but distributed meshes are opening new frontiers for LLM infrastructure.
