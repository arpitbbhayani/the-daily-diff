Ever wonder why some people love AI, while others find it a hellish ordeal? The "AI paradox" can be explained by "centaurs" and "reverse centaurs".

A centaur is a human assisted by a machine, enhancing capabilities. Think of it as a human controlling a powerful tool.

A reverse centaur is a machine using a human as its assistant. This is where AI's promise turns sour, like when chatbots hallucinate content that a human then has to clean up or takes the blame for.

The distinction is critical for engineers designing AI systems. Are we empowering users or turning them into cogs in a machine's workflow?

Design AI that serves humans, not the other way around.
