Many European organizations are banning personal messaging apps for work. This trend is driven by critical concerns over data control, record-keeping, compliance, and digital sovereignty.
Imagine losing years of critical business communications because they live on personal devices or uncontrolled platforms. The absence of an official audit trail can be devastating in legal or regulatory disputes.
This is not just about privacy; it is about an organization's ability to maintain a verifiable record of its operations and ensure data integrity.
For senior engineers, this highlights the growing importance of secure, compliant internal communication tools as part of robust system design and operational best practices.
