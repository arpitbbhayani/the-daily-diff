Deriving accurate priorities in the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) is often computationally demanding, especially when dealing with complex, non-linear optimization models. Traditional methods can struggle with theoretical robustness and efficiency.
This research introduces POO-LPSP, a novel approach that leverages a Parallel Osprey Optimization Algorithm (POOA) to tackle these challenges. It efficiently solves the revised Least Penalty-Squared Prioritization (LPSP) models, enhancing the reliability of priority derivation.
The integration of a bio-inspired metaheuristic with parallel processing significantly reduces the computational complexity, making it practical for real-world decision-makers. The framework's utility was validated through a Generative AI (GAI) vendor selection problem, demonstrating its practical application.
This offers a robust and efficient alternative to conventional methods like Saaty's Eigen system, particularly valuable for engineers needing to make complex, data-driven decisions where computational efficiency and reliability are paramount.
