Document parsing with AI is notoriously difficult due to data scarcity. Infinity-Parser2 tackles this head-on with a clever, scalable data-synthesis pipeline.
This multimodal model, combining controllable rendering with iterative refinement, created Infinity-Doc2-5M - a 5-million-sample bilingual corpus.
It uses a verifiable, multi-task reward system, unifying perception, structure, and reasoning for document parsing, layout analysis, table parsing, and more.
Infinity-Parser2-Pro achieves state-of-the-art 87.6% on olmOCR-Bench. This is a significant leap for robust, generalizable document understanding.
