Building truly intelligent AI agents requires more than just reactive decision-making; it demands robust temporal planning and the ability to hunt for contradictions within dynamic, incomplete knowledge. This is a formidable challenge for agents operating in open-world environments.

PIE-APT offers a unified framework that works natively with highly expressive Description Logic. It employs an incremental direct-derivation abduction method, cleverly avoiding combinatorial search by extracting missing premises via direct refutation.

This approach significantly enhances agents' capabilities for complex reasoning, allowing them to navigate incomplete information and maintain logical consistency. It is a major step towards more sophisticated agentic AI.
