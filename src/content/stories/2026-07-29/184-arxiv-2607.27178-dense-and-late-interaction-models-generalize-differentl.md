Building state-of-the-art retrieval models for RAG, multilingual search, or code search can feel like a black box. This paper, "DenseOn with the LateOn", cracks it open with a fully transparent, end-to-end recipe.

It not only presents two powerful 149M-parameter models, DenseOn and LateOn, that achieve new state-of-the-art results on BEIR, but it also provides all the curated datasets and training code. Crucially, it explores how English supervision transfers to multilingual retrieval through translate-train and reveals fascinating differences in generalization between dense and late-interaction architectures across unseen languages and scripts.

This is an invaluable resource if you are designing retrieval systems for complex, real-world applications. You gain not just models, but the knowledge to build them from the ground up, with specific insights into scaling and multilingual performance.
