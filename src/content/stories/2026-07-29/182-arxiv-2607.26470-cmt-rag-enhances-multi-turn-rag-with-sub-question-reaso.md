Building truly intelligent multi-turn, multi-hop RAG systems is challenging because LLMs struggle to track long-range dependencies across conversational turns. Most RAG systems treat dialogue history as unstructured text, making it hard to recover specific reasoning steps.

CMT-RAG introduces a game-changing approach: it aligns conversational memory with retrieval by representing dialogue context as sub-question-level reasoning traces. These structured traces are stored in a session-level DAG, allowing future turns to efficiently recover prior reasoning and evidence.

This framework enables complex information-seeking conversations, where an LLM can effectively follow up on earlier steps and combine evidence from multiple sources. It is a critical leap forward for building robust, context-aware AI agents.
