Financial documents are notoriously difficult for RAG systems due to their scale and hybrid text-table structures. This paper introduces the Hierarchical Reranker, a RAG framework specifically engineered to tackle these challenges head-on.

It integrates three key innovations: Pre-Retrieval Optimization for cleaner queries, a Hierarchical Reranker Architecture for precision, and smart Long-Context Management to maintain accuracy with extensive inputs. This is not just theoretical; the system achieved an impressive 0.7918 NDCG@20 and superior factual consistency on financial benchmarks.

For engineers building RAG applications for demanding, complex datasets, this provides a deployable blueprint. You will find highly actionable strategies for improving retrieval performance and generative reliability, paving the way for more robust applied AI in critical domains.
