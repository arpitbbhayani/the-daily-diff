Detecting AI agents that automate browsers is not a binary human-vs-bot problem. Traditional classifiers structurally confuse agents with humans, misclassifying nearly 40 percent of agent sessions.

This paper unveils an architectural flaw in how we classify online traffic, showing that a three-class framework (human, bot, AI agent) is essential. With just two behavioral features, mouse_event_rate and teleport_click_ratio, you can achieve 100 percent agent recall, even against sophisticated evasion techniques.

This is a game-changer for anyone building or deploying web-interacting AI agents; understanding these signatures is crucial for robust system design.
