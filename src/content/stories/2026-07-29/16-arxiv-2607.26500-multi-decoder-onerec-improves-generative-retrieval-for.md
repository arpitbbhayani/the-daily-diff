Industrial recommender systems often rely on fragmented retrieval routes, each designed for a specific objective, leading to complexity and suboptimal candidate pools. Multi-Decoder OneRec offers a unified and controllable generative retrieval framework to solve this.

It combines shared representations across all objectives with isolated, parameter-efficient LoRA experts for each target. Crucially, it employs gradient routing during training and Multi-Decoder Constrained Beam Search during inference to ensure coordinated candidate generation without entanglement.

Deployed at Kwai, this framework yielded impressive results: 0.37% gain in app usage time, 0.19% in Day-7 retained users, and 2.09% in new-content cold-start. This demonstrates that generative retrieval can indeed achieve shared modeling with objective-specific control and complementary candidate generation at scale.
