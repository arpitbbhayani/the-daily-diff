Ever struggled with the fundamental misalignment between efficient indexing and accurate ranking in large-scale retrieval systems? Instagram has unveiled 'OneShot,' an end-to-end framework that redefines how we think about this problem.

OneShot introduces an "index-in-ranking" paradigm. It natively aligns index learning with ranking objectives, overcoming the traditional dot-product bottleneck that limits expressiveness. This is not just theoretical; it is fully deployed in Instagram's short-video recommendation system.

The results are striking: significant wins in user engagement and time spent, a 20 percent recall gain at operational ranking volume, and a 10x efficiency improvement at equivalent recall. This provides a blueprint for building next-generation retrieval architectures that demand both speed and precision at immense scale.
