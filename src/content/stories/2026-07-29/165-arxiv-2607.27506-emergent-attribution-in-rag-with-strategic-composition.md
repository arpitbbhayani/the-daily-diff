Building powerful RAG systems usually means big models and big costs, but this paper showcases an approach with "minimalist RAG" using just a 335M embedding model and a 1B parameter SLM. The results are striking.

The B1ade architecture achieves strong QA benchmarks while being significantly smaller. A particularly cool finding is "emergent attribution": the SLM starts citing retrieved passages without explicit supervision, simply because the RL training rewards optimized for answer similarity. This is a game-changer for building trustworthy RAG systems more simply.

For engineers grappling with RAG costs and reliability, this work demonstrates that strategic model composition and clever reward design can yield highly effective and resource-efficient solutions. It is a powerful reminder that smart engineering can outperform brute force scaling.
