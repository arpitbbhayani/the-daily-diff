Training and serving large language models efficiently is a major challenge, especially when pushing for lower precision like 4-bit (FP4). A systematic study reveals that the biggest hurdle in end-to-end FP4 Reinforcement Learning post-training for LLMs is not training-side quantization error, but rather rollout activation quantization due to outliers underflowing to zero.

Counterintuitively, simply restoring training policy to higher precision while keeping rollout in FP4 actually worsens accuracy due to rollout-training mismatch. The solution proposed here is Rollout Residual Quantization (Rollout-ResQ) paired with the HiFloat4 (HiF4) format. Rollout-ResQ adds a lightweight, hardware-friendly residual correction to the FP4 rollout matmul.

This approach recovers most lost precision, bringing fully quantized FP4 RL accuracy within 1.1% of BF16 for Qwen2.5-3B and Qwen2.5-Math-7B. This is a game-changer for deploying powerful LLMs with vastly reduced computational and memory footprints, making them more accessible and cost-effective.

This innovation unlocks efficient, high-performance LLM deployment.
