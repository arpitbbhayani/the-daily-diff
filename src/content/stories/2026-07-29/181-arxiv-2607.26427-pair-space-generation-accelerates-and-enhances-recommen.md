Generative reranking in recommender systems is powerful but often constrained by latency. Traditional autoregressive methods have complexity growing linearly with list length, limiting exploration under strict serving budgets.

A novel approach, Pair-Space Generation (PSG), reframes the problem by elevating the generation atom from individual items to ordered item pairs. This simple yet profound change drastically improves efficiency, achieving 1.83x speedup in industrial settings while generating only half the tokens.

Deployed on Kuaishou, PSG delivered a 0.178% lift in per-user stay time for over 400 million daily active users. This is not just a theoretical gain; it is a battle-tested architecture that enhances both speed and user engagement.

Reranking just got a major performance boost.
