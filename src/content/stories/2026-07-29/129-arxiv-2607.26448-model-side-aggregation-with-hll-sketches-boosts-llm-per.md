Long-context LLMs struggle with basic aggregation tasks like counting distinct items. A brilliant new approach integrates mergeable HyperLogLog (HLL) sketch states directly 'model-side' to solve this.

This allows LLMs to perform cardinality estimation and grouped statistics reliably, bypassing their inherent unreliability for non-additive operations. The HLL states are compact, mergeable across context segments, and avoid expensive generate-execute-return cycles. It is a highly practical architectural pattern for building robust LLM applications, showing a mean relative error of only 1.6% on one million records.
