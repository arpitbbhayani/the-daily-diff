Evaluating large generative models is a massive bottleneck. You cannot afford to run full benchmarks every time, but how do you know a subset is truly representative and that your performance estimates are reliable? Current methods often require you to guess the "coreset" size.

BayesAME solves this by introducing a sequential Bayesian framework that automatically determines the optimal coreset size. It models performance uncertainties and iteratively selects the most informative items, ensuring reliable estimation with minimal computational cost.

This is not just an incremental improvement; it is a fundamental shift in how we approach MLOps, saving significant resources while guaranteeing evaluation quality for your applied AI systems.
