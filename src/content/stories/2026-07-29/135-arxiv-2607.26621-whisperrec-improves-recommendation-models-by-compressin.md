Integrating LLM reasoning into recommendation systems often means trading off performance for better contextual understanding. Traditional Chain-of-Thought (CoT) generates verbose rationales, crippling inference throughput for real-time applications.

WhisperRec offers a game-changing solution: latent reasoning. Instead of generating explicit rationales, it compresses teacher-generated CoT into learnable latent reasoning tokens. This allows the model to "reason" in a compact latent space, delivering the benefits of CoT without the massive inference overhead.

The results are striking: a 17.44 percent improvement in SID@64 and over 10x higher online inference throughput on an industrial dataset. This approach solves a critical problem for engineers looking to deploy LLM-powered recommendation models at scale, enabling deeper reasoning capabilities without sacrificing system efficiency.
