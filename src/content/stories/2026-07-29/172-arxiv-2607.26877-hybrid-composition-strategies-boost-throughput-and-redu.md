Optimizing workflows for extreme-scale data processing is a black art. Simply breaking tasks into fine-grained units often leads to massive overhead, while coarse-grained units can underutilize resources.

This research introduces a novel simulation framework to explore *hybrid workflow composition*. It dynamically balances taskset independence with execution grouping to mitigate overheads and maximize utilization.

The results are compelling: up to a 3.8x throughput increase and a 14.9x reduction in network overhead for petabyte-scale data. This provides a rigorous foundation for automated workflow synthesis in your next-generation scientific or data pipeline.
