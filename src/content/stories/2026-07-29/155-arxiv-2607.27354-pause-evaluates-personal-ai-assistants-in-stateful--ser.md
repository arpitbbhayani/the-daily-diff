Current benchmarks for personal AI assistants often fragment service contexts or abstract away user state, missing the real-world complexity. A new benchmark, PAUSE, aims to fix this by evaluating agents in unified service environments that demand stateful reasoning and constraint-aware interactions.

PAUSE challenges agents to coordinate actions across heterogeneous user-owned resources, maintain consistency with environment state, and respect authorization constraints over multi-turn interactions. It even incorporates realistic user simulation for a truly user-centric evaluation.

This reveals that even state-of-the-art proprietary models often fail to reach 70 percent task completion. For senior engineers building next-gen personal assistants, PAUSE highlights the critical need to master stateful reasoning and cross-service coordination, providing a clear roadmap for more robust and reliable agent design.
