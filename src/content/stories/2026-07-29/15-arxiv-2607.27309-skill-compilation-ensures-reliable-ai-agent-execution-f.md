Agent skills defined in prose are notoriously unreliable. The model re-derives control flow on every run, leading to mandated steps being skipped and inconsistent behavior. On average, prose agents perform only 56 percent of their own mandated steps.

SIGIL introduces Skill Compilation, turning prose skills into executable, typed harnesses using AG-IR, an agentic intermediate representation. This shifts from model-owned cognition of control flow to code-owned mechanism.

Compiled harnesses execute 86 percent of mandated steps, completing the full procedure 2.3 times more often, and require 0.58 times the tokens. This is a model-independent guarantee of reliability, a significant leap forward for production-grade LLM agents.
