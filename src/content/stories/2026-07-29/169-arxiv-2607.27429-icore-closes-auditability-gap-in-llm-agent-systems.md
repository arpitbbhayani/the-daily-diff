Emergent cooperation in LLM-agent systems is powerful, but how do you audit it to ensure reliability and detect hidden flaws? This paper introduces iCORE, a novel framework that brings much-needed visibility to complex agent collaboration.

iCORE provides an "Integrated Cooperation-Obligation REpresentation" by linking observable interactions (cooperation graph) with evolving work and assignments (obligation graph) via an audit map. This allows for certifying two crucial properties: work soundness and agent-assignment stability.

The results are compelling: iCORE-Audit leads to absolute trajectory quality improvements of 11.5% and 26.4% in controlled and real-LLM execution modes, respectively. For senior engineers building multi-agent systems, having a structured way to understand and verify agent behavior is critical for moving beyond experimental setups to reliable production systems.
