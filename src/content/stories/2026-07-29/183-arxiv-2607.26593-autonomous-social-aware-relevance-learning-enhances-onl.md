Achieving high relevance in social search, where language is informal and community-specific, is incredibly hard. Manual data labeling is slow and subjective. Tencent's QQ search team built ASARL, an autonomous multi-agent framework to tackle this head-on.

ASARL uses a collaborative system of agents: a ReasonAgent generates interpretable relevance labels, a CriticAgent validates consistency, and a GenAgent augments long-tail data with synthetic pairs. This automates and scales data curation dramatically.

Combined with a three-stage training process, ASARL delivered significant improvements in both offline metrics and online user engagement on QQ. This is a powerful demonstration of how agentic AI can automate complex data workflows and drive real product impact.
