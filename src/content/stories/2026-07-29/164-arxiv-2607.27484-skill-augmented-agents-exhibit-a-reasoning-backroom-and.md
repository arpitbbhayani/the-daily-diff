When an LLM agent says it used a skill, did it *really* use it, or was it just "skill theater"? This paper introduces BACKTRACE, a groundbreaking framework that exposes a pervasive "Reasoning Backroom" problem in skill-augmented agents.

The problem is that visible reasoning or agent attribution often do not reflect genuine causal influence. BACKTRACE addresses this by pairing skill-conditioned answers with no-skill counterfactuals and intervening on skill meaning and content to assess true impact.

This research is essential for anyone building or evaluating sophisticated AI agents. It provides a robust methodology to ensure agents are not just mimicking skill use, but are truly integrating and leveraging their capabilities for effective decision-making.
