Agent self-evolution typically focuses on how to act, but what if an agent could learn to diagnose its own blind spots? This paper introduces SkillMentor, a groundbreaking approach where LLM agents learn to discover what they do not know.

SkillMentor trains a dedicated 'Mentor' policy using reinforcement learning. This Mentor generates diagnostic tasks, identifies recurrent failure modes, and then curates these into reusable corrective skills. Critically, this self-improvement happens without human supervision or directly updating the executor's weights.

This novel capability enables agents to dramatically improve their performance, as demonstrated by a 44.2 percent average improvement across benchmarks. It is a significant leap towards truly autonomous AI, moving beyond execution to internal reflection and self-correction, fundamentally changing how we think about agent learning.
