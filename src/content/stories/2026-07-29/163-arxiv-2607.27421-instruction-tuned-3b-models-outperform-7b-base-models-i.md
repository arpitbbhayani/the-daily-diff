Selecting the right open-weight LLM for production intent classification can feel like a guessing game. This systematic evaluation of 41 models across various families and parameter ranges provides much-needed clarity for practitioners facing compute, latency, and robustness constraints.

The findings are compelling: instruction-tuned 3B models can often outperform larger 7B base models. The study also reveals that some widely used benchmarks, such as SNIPS, are now saturated and no longer effectively discriminate among current models.

This research offers actionable guidance, backed by data, on which models to consider for deployment. It helps you avoid over-provisioning and makes informed decisions about model selection.
