LLM agents often hit a ceiling because their underlying 'harness' - the fixed tools, context, and workflow - remains static, failing to adapt from past errors. Living-Harness introduces a paradigm shift: a self-evolving agent harness.

This system converts every completed trajectory and evaluator signal into actionable evidence, prompting bounded updates to the harness. It creates two forms of procedural knowledge: episodic memory, recording failure patterns and recovery actions, and a state graph mapping states and repair edges. This dynamic update process means the agent's guiding logic continuously improves over time.

Living-Harness shows significant performance gains, improving Pass@1 over strong interactive baselines by 10 percentage points across various environments. This marks a critical step towards agents that truly learn from experience and become more resilient, moving beyond static configurations to genuinely adaptive AI systems.
