Training coding agents to construct complete programs from scratch is a significant hurdle for LLMs. Current methods often lack scalable, whole-life-cycle training environments.

MindForge introduces an automated pipeline to convert open-source command-line programs into source-free environments, using only compiled executables and documentation. By fine-tuning smaller models like Qwen3.6-27B on these generated trajectories, its ProgramBench pass rate jumped from 37.98 percent to 49.51 percent.

This not only matches larger frontier models but demonstrates significant absolute gains across seven unseen benchmarks. It is a game-changer for efficiently scaling and empowering coding agents.
