Reranking in recommendation systems often struggles with a fundamental trade-off: autoregressive models are sequential and slow, while non-autoregressive models lack coordination and can produce redundant items. A new framework, DIRECTOR, offers an elegant solution.

DIRECTOR introduces transport-optimized retrieval to generate dynamic indices for all target positions in parallel, significantly boosting efficiency. During training, it uses entropy-regularized optimal transport to provide conflict-aware supervision, ensuring global coordination without iterative search.

This approach avoids the premature pruning of promising permutations seen in greedy search and eliminates duplicate selections. Extensive offline and online experiments show DIRECTOR consistently outperforms strong baselines, marking a substantial step forward for high-performance recommendation systems.
