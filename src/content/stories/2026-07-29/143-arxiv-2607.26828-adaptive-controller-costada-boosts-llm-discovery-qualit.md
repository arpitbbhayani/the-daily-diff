Optimizing large language model usage for scientific or algorithmic discovery is often a budget-constrained problem. Most adaptive discovery controllers are "cost-blind," assigning credit based only on score progress, ignoring the varying token costs of different actions. This can lead to significant wastage and suboptimal outcomes.

This paper introduces CostAda, a game-changing, cost-calibrated adaptive controller. It integrates the actual token cost of prompt length, retries, and guidance calls directly into its decision-making, valuing frontier progress relative to the realized action cost and the remaining budget.

The impact is substantial: CostAda achieves the quality of the strongest baseline at full budget using only half the tokens on twelve of sixteen benchmarks. It also delivers the strongest mean final quality on all eight benchmarks under GLM-5 and GPT-5.4.

For any senior engineer leveraging LLMs for complex search or discovery tasks, this research offers a highly practical and actionable paradigm shift. It is not just about using smarter models, but about building smarter, budget-aware control systems around them to maximize value.
