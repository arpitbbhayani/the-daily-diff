Distributed AI workloads on hundreds of accelerators often hit a communication bottleneck. Existing libraries are buffer-centric, leading to redundant copies and registration costs.

StrataCL revolutionizes this by introducing zero-redundancy, fabric-native communication. It uses 'registration-on-allocation' for direct user-buffer access and hardware-specific optimizations like NPU-core partitioning and SDMA offloading.

The results are compelling: up to 1.9x faster LLM inference throughput and 1.4x faster training iteration times. This is a game-changer for anyone building or operating high-scale LLM infrastructure.
