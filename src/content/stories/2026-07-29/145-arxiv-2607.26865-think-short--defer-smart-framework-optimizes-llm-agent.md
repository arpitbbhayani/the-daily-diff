Deploying LLM agents on edge devices means constantly battling compute limits and ensuring reliability. A new framework, Think Short, Defer Smart (TSDS), offers a compelling solution by intelligently managing reasoning budgets and uncertainty.

TSDS integrates a lightweight convergence probe that halts on-device reasoning once an action has stabilized. Alongside this, a perplexity-based deferral rule escalates uncertain actions to a more powerful cloud-side model only when necessary.

The results are impressive: TSDS reduces per-episode thinking compute by 43%-73% across various benchmarks while maintaining certified reward and cloud-call rate guarantees. This is not just theoretical; it is a practical blueprint for efficient, robust edge agent deployment.

Smart deferral, not just smaller models, is key to practical edge AI.
