The performance of LLM agents often hinges on a well-tuned "harness," but evolving these harnesses reliably is a huge challenge. DREvo tackles this head-on by teaching agents to intelligently distill and recalibrate their historical experience.

Traditional self-evolution struggles with unstable guidance, but DREvo introduces function-level evidence anchoring, state-dependent evidence recalibration, and role-conditioned search intent distillation. This means agents can dynamically re-evaluate past experiences and translate them into actionable future improvements.

The results are impressive: DREvo shows smoother evolution trajectories and significant accuracy gains (16.2% on domain reasoning, 14.2% on agentic tasks) over baselines, even with limited evolution budgets. For anyone building production-grade LLM agents, understanding how to make harnesses truly self-evolving and stable is a critical skill.
