LLMs can suffer from a subtle but critical bias: "Narrative Anchoring." This means identical clinical facts, presented in different sociolinguistic registers, can lead to divergent diagnostic outputs, even without explicit demographic markers.

This is a distinct failure mode from traditional demographic bias. The paper introduces NarrativeShield, a three-agent pipeline designed to structurally extract and verify clinical facts before diagnostic reasoning. This approach effectively disentangles content from style.

The results are compelling: NarrativeShield reduces the Narrative Anchoring Gap to near-zero, significantly improving the stability of decisions. For engineers building high-stakes AI applications, especially in domains like healthcare, this offers a practical and actionable pattern for architecting more robust and unbiased systems.

Fact verification via agentic systems is key for dependable AI.
