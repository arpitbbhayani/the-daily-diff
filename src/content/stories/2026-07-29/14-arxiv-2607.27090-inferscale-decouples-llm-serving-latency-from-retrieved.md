Serving personalized LLMs with long, persistent contexts typically means repeatedly prefilling the same information, leading to massive time-to-first-token (TTFT) latency. This is a critical bottleneck.

InferScale offers a groundbreaking solution: GPU-native KV injection. Instead of re-prefilling, it precomputes memory fact KV representations, stores them on the GPU, and injects them directly into vLLM's paged cache.

This approach, combined with innovations like Chunked RoPE, reduces TTFT by 72-79% and boosts throughput by 3.7-4.5x. It decouples serving latency from retrieved context size, fundamentally changing how you can scale personalized and agentic LLM applications.
