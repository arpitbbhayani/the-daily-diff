LLM agents can tackle complex office tasks, but are they actually cost-effective and performant? A new benchmark, OmegaUse-OfficeVal, tackles this by evaluating agents on long-horizon office-suite workflows with task-level economic grounding.

The benchmark, comprising 100 practitioner-derived tasks averaging 2.32 hours of human labor, provides critical insights. It shows that while frontier LLMs are significantly cheaper and faster than humans, they have not yet achieved human-level deliverable quality.

This is a crucial reality check for anyone building or deploying AI agents: raw cost savings do not automatically translate to equivalent value without addressing significant quality gaps. The open-sourced benchmark provides a powerful tool to measure progress.
