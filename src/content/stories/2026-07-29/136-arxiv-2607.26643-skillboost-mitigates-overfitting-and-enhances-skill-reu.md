A major hurdle for LLM agents accumulating experience is skill overfitting. Without careful management, agents learn from limited trajectories and struggle to generalize, or unconstrained exploration erases previously learned capabilities.

SkillBoost introduces a powerful three-stage framework to mitigate this. It combines structured exploitation to localize observed failures, prior-guided exploration leveraging the LLM's inherent knowledge for diverse repair candidates, and a verified acceptance mechanism that only commits improvements within a regression bound.

This constrained exploration-exploitation approach ensures agents accumulate robust, generalizable skills. Experiments across 23 model-benchmark configurations demonstrate state-of-the-art performance, outperforming both human-crafted and LLM-generated skills. This is a critical advancement for engineers designing agents that learn and adapt reliably in production environments.
