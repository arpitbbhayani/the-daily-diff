Are multimodal LLMs truly 'seeing' and using intermediate visual states for reasoning, or are they just generating them for show? See2Think introduces a crucial evaluation framework to answer this question.

It goes beyond just checking final answers, diagnosing how visual states are generated, rendered, and actually utilized by the model. The framework includes a benchmark with 1,200 visually dependent problems and controlled inference settings to observe model behavior under varying visual feedback.

The findings are insightful: model performance is highly dependent on faithful rendering, and high feedback uptake does not always translate to accuracy. This is vital for anyone building or evaluating advanced multimodal AI agents, shifting focus from outputs to mechanistic understanding.
