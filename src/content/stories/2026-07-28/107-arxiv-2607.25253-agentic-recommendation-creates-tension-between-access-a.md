The rise of LLM-based user agents is fundamentally changing online recommendation. We are moving from platform-centric experiences to 'agentic recommendation markets' where user agents ask, and platforms compete.

This creates a fascinating tension between access and attention. Platforms adopt strategic play, like selectively positive explanations, to capture agent attention. However, when user agents incorporate feedback mechanisms, the dynamic shifts, improving user utility and reining in platform biases.

For anyone designing future agentic systems or large-scale platforms, this research offers crucial insights into emergent market behaviors and the critical role of mechanism design. It is not just about the models; it is about how they interact and shape complex economies.
