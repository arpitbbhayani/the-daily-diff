Ensuring the safety and robustness of large language models in production is paramount, and automated red-teaming is proving to be a game-changer. GPT-Red, a new agent, discovers novel prompt injection attacks against frontier LLMs through a scalable self-play algorithm.

This agent was trained on realistic red-teaming environments at an unprecedented scale, making it the largest LLM safety training run ever documented. It significantly outperforms human red-teamers and breaks past models, eventually training GPT-5.6 to be exceptionally robust.

GPT-Red's ability to generalize to held-out environments and defender models highlights a critical self-improvement flywheel: as new GPT models become more robust, they provide better learning signals for even stronger red-teamer agents. This is a powerful feedback loop for security.

This work exemplifies how advanced AI agent capabilities can directly enhance the engineering practices around deploying and securing LLMs at scale.
