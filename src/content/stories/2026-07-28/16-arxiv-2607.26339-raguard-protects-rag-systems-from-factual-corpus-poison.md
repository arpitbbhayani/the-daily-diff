Data poisoning is a critical threat to Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems. RAGuard introduces a powerful, layered defense framework designed to protect against maliciously injected passages that can manipulate retrieved evidence and, consequently, LLM outputs.

This framework combines two potent strategies: adversarial fine-tuning for dense retrievers, which teaches them to downrank malicious content, and a novel Zero-Knowledge Inference Patch (ZKIP). ZKIP is a game-changer because it is a label-free, black-box filter that does not need poison labels or access to model internals.

ZKIP works by performing leave-one-out decodes for each retrieved document, scoring it by the semantic shift and output-entropy change its removal causes. This approach drove attack success rates to zero in experiments while preserving recall. For any engineer deploying RAG in production, understanding RAGuard is essential for building robust and trustworthy AI systems.
