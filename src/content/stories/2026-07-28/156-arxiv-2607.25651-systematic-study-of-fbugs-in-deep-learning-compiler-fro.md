Deep learning compilers are critical infrastructure for LLMs, but their frontend bugs (fBugs) are often severe and understudied. This paper provides the first systematic empirical look at these issues within TorchDynamo, the default compiler for PyTorch 2.

The researchers used an LLM-aided methodology to analyze 123 existing fBugs, building a comprehensive taxonomy of 7 root cause categories and 15 subcategories. This deep dive offers invaluable insights for anyone building or optimizing DL frameworks.

Even more impressively, they leveraged LLMs to generate targeted, root cause-aware test cases, which led to the discovery of 23 previously unknown bugs, 15 of which were confirmed in recent releases. This demonstrates a powerful, practical application of AI to improve software quality and reliability in complex systems.
