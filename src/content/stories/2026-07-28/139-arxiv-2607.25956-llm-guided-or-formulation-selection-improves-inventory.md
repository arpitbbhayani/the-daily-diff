Solving complex optimization problems, like multi-warehouse inventory allocation, often means choosing the right mathematical formulation. But what if no single formulation works best across all scenarios? This paper introduces a groundbreaking LLM framework that dynamically selects the optimal operations research (OR) formulation for each instance.

The secret sauce is a solver-guided reinforcement learning approach. By using supervised fine-tuning, identity preference optimization, and group relative policy optimization with real-world solver feedback, the LLM learns to pick the best strategy on the fly.

This approach delivered incredible results at JD.com, one of China's largest e-retailers. Hit Ratio@1 jumped from 21.45% to 50.42%, and overall allocation accuracy improved by 12.57 percentage points. It is not just about LLMs generating text; it is about them becoming powerful, adaptive decision-makers for core business logic.
