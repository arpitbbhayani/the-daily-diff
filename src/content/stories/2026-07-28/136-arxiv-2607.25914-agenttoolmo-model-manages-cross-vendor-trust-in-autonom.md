Autonomous networks operating at Levels 4-5 require AI agents to invoke tools across vendor boundaries without human oversight. This presents a massive trust management problem: how do you prevent cascading failures when a tool from one vendor is compromised, and agents from another continue using it?

This paper introduces AgentToolMO, a proposed 3GPP NRM information model that offers a standardized mechanism for cross-vendor trust visibility. It includes a formally defined trust state machine with provable enforcement, damped cascade propagation, and cross-vendor notifications via existing Management Services interfaces.

Simulation evaluations show a dramatic reduction in blast radius from hours of undetected propagation to near-real-time containment. Cascade convergence is guaranteed, and notification scaling is sub-linear. This framework leverages existing infrastructure, providing a clear standardization pathway for trustworthy multi-vendor autonomous network management.

This is not just a theoretical concept; it is a blueprint for making multi-agent, multi-vendor distributed systems truly robust and reliable at scale.
