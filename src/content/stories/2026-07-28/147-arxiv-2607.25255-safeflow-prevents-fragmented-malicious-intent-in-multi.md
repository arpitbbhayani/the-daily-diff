Multi-agent systems face a critical security flaw: malicious intent can splinter, evading single-agent detection.

SafeFlow, a novel defense framework, tackles this by formalizing malicious propagation as a 'semantic information-flow problem'. It attaches structured semantic taints to root requests, propagates them through a dynamic collaboration graph, and performs workflow-level validation before any irreversible actions are committed.

This is a game-changer for anyone building production-grade agent systems. Traditional security measures struggle when harmful objectives are fragmented. SafeFlow provides a concrete, architectural solution to maintain global risk context, preventing privacy harms or unsafe actions.

The ability to keep risk visible across delegation boundaries is paramount for robust agent system design. This approach offers a powerful mechanism to ensure your multi-agent applications operate securely.
