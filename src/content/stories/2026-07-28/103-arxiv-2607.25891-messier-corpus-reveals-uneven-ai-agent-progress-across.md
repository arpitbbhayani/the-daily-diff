Evaluating AI agents feels like a fragmented mess across dozens of benchmarks and scoring rules. Messier is here to fix that, offering a unified corpus of nearly a million records from 30 benchmarks and 714 agents.

This massive dataset standardizes everything from models to tasks, revealing critical insights: "function calling" is saturated, "programming" is improving fastest, and "enterprise workflows" remain the hardest. Messier also exposes how strict all-pass aggregations can hide real progress.

If you are serious about understanding agent capabilities, auditing benchmarks, or conducting fine-grained failure analysis, this infrastructure is a game-changer. It provides a standardized lens to measure and compare agent progress like never before.
