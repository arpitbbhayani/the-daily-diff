Deploying deep neural networks on resource-constrained hardware is often bottlenecked by mixed-precision quantization, which is typically disconnected from the compiler. This leads to suboptimal configurations and severe runtime penalties.

CONQuER offers a game-changing approach: it shifts quantization into the compiler pipeline at the TOSA level, enabling intelligent, hardware-aware configuration. It combines an NSGA-II evolutionary algorithm with a dual-surrogate prescreening engine to efficiently navigate the vast search space.

The true innovation lies in its online calibrator. CONQuER executes strong candidate policies on actual hardware, feeding those metrics back to align the surrogate models with true hardware behavior during the evolutionary search. This ensures optimal policies are truly hardware-dependent.

The results are stunning, demonstrating up to 12.19x faster inference with minimal accuracy drop. This is a critical advancement for anyone building performant AI infrastructure, proving that deep compiler integration and real-time hardware feedback are paramount for next-gen model deployment.
