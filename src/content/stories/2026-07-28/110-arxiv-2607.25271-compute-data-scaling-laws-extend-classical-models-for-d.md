Are you hitting a data wall in LLM pretraining? The classical scaling laws, which assume infinite data, might be misleading your resource allocation. A new framework called Compute-Data (CD) scaling laws sheds light on the true economics of training when data becomes a bottleneck.

This research introduces a 'token-effectiveness' function that quantifies the value of recycled or paraphrased tokens compared to fresh ones. It reveals that this value is not constant but diminishes with model size and data expansion, partitioning training into compute-bound, data-bound, and model-bound regimes.

This work offers critical insights for anyone designing or optimizing LLM training pipelines, providing a more realistic lens through which to plan data strategies and compute investments. Do not just scale compute; understand your data's true diminishing returns.
