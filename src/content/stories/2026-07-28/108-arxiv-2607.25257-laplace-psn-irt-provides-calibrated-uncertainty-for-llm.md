Are LLM leaderboard rankings as definitive as they appear? This paper challenges that assumption, revealing that many pairwise comparisons among models are not statistically distinguishable when uncertainty is properly quantified.

Laplace-PSN-IRT introduces a post-hoc Laplace approximation to neural Item Response Theory models, providing calibrated uncertainty over model ability and item difficulty without retraining. This allows for credible intervals and probabilistic comparisons, moving beyond mere point estimates.

For engineers selecting LLMs based on benchmarks, this means more robust decision-making. You will learn to identify when a seemingly higher-ranked model is genuinely better versus just statistically indistinguishable, fundamentally changing how you interpret evaluation results.
