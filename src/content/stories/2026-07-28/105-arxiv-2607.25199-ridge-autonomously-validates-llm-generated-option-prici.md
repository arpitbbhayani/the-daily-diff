Validating LLM-generated code is a huge hurdle, especially in high-stakes domains. RIDGE offers an autonomous framework that tackles this head-on, not just by testing, but by *discovering* new validation methodologies.

This framework subjects LLM-generated option pricing implementations to structured no-arbitrage, stress tests, and consistency checks. The system accumulates knowledge and systematically refines its approach, removing defects and, in some cases, leading to completely new pricing methods.

This is more than just conventional software testing; it is an agentic approach to ensuring mathematical consistency and numerical stability. For any senior engineer grappling with the reliability of AI-generated artifacts, this paper provides a blueprint for building self-improving validation systems.
