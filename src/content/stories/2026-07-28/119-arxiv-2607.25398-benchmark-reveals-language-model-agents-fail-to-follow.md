Getting LLM agents to reliably follow long, complex instructions is a major hurdle for enterprise adoption. Most benchmarks do not truly test if an agent is constrained by a standing policy document, leading to agents that "hallucinate" compliance.

Introducing HANDBOOK.md: a rigorous benchmark with 65 agentic tasks simulating real enterprise environments, complete with file workspaces and mock services. Agents must adhere to 20-124 page policy handbooks, and are graded by 824 programmatic criteria.

The results are stark: even frontier models struggle, with the best achieving only a 36.2 percent pass rate under strict grading. Common failure patterns include agents letting plausible in-environment requests override standing policy or losing track of rule details over long horizons. This highlights a deep challenge in agentic AI.

This benchmark is an essential tool for engineers aiming to build truly reliable and compliant AI agents. It forces us to confront the reality that more context does not automatically mean better adherence without explicit design.
