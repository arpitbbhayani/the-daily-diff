Your AI agents' proprietary skills might not be as secret as you think. This paper exposes "Skill Leakage," demonstrating how valuable agent procedures can be reconstructed purely from observable execution trajectories, even from benign queries.

The SigLeak framework, a black-box approach, exploits recurring behavioral signatures. It contrasts skill-enabled and skill-disabled trajectories on diverse diagnostic tasks to iteratively refine a reconstructed skill. This means that hiding the internal artifacts does not conceal the behavioral effects, which form a side channel.

Across various scenarios, models, and agent frameworks, SigLeak significantly outperforms baselines, achieving high success rates and semantic similarity scores. This research highlights a critical intellectual property and security vulnerability that every engineer working with AI agents must understand.

Protect your agent's secret sauce; its behavior might be giving it away.
