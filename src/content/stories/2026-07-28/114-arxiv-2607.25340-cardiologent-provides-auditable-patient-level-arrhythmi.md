Building robust AI agents that can handle complex, high-stakes decision-making is a grand challenge. Cardiologent presents a multi-agent system that steps up, moving beyond simple rhythm detection to patient-level arrhythmia assessment and management.

This system is a masterclass in agentic design. It employs multiple agents: one for ECG, one for photoplethysmogram, each grounding its window readings in measured features. These readings are then assembled into a patient's rhythm profile, reasoned against clinical guidelines, and critically, checked by a separate critic agent. This multi-layered validation ensures auditability, a critical factor for real-world medical applications.

The significant finding is that Cardiologent scores highest across integrated diagnosis, clinical significance, and urgency under both cardiologists and an at-scale LLM judge. This demonstrates how a well-architected multi-agent system can achieve human-level judgment in complex scenarios, extending beyond what typical single-task LLMs can provide.

This work offers invaluable lessons for designing any applied AI system requiring grounded reasoning, multi-modal input synthesis, and transparent, auditable decision paths.
