Automating regulatory compliance is undergoing a paradigm shift with agentic program synthesis.

ARCHER, an Agentic Rule and Compliance Harness, generates auditable verification code directly from regulatory Codes of Practice. This test-driven, deterministically orchestrated multi-agent system provides transparency and scalability that traditional Automated Compliance Checkers lack.

The paper dives deep into evaluating different agentic harness designs across various LLM backbones, including self-hosted open-weights models. It shows that an open-weights model, when paired with ARCHER, can achieve 97.8% of frontier-API accuracy at a quarter of the cost.

This offers crucial insights for engineering managers and architects. You can achieve high accuracy in complex rule verification with open-source LLMs, making data-sovereign compliance practical and cost-effective. It is a powerful example of leveraging multi-agent systems for robust, verifiable code generation.
