Training smaller LLMs to excel at complex reasoning tasks often uses on-policy distillation, but it hits a wall with "prefix failure" \u2013 once the student model veers off course, it struggles to recover. "Pass the Baton: Trajectory-Relayed On-Policy Distillation" (Relay-OPD) offers an elegant solution.

Relay-OPD identifies a "teacher-student continuation asymmetry" and uses a label-free handoff trigger. When the student goes awry, the teacher model temporarily takes over, generating a correct "teacher leg" of the trajectory. The student then resumes, learning from this corrected path.

This dynamic intervention dramatically improves mathematical reasoning benchmarks, outperforming standard on-policy distillation by over five percentage points and cutting training trajectory length by more than 50 percent. It is a powerful technique for engineers looking to make their LLMs smarter and their training pipelines more efficient.
