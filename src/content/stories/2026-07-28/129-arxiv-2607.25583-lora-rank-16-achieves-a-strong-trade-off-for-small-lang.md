Optimizing LLMs for resource-constrained environments just got a practical blueprint, proving you do not always need massive models for efficiency gains.

A controlled study on a 60M-parameter T5-small model for text-to-SQL reveals precise trade-offs for LoRA rank and quantization. They found that LoRA with rank 16 recovers 59.6% exact-match accuracy (just 11.6% shy of full fine-tuning) while using less than 1% of trainable parameters and 31% less peak GPU memory.

Pushing rank beyond 16 yielded no measurable accuracy improvement, highlighting a clear sweet spot. For extreme memory savings, QLoRA with INT8 and NF4 quantization achieved comparable accuracy at a dramatically lower memory cost of only 0.60 GB.

This is not just theoretical; these are system-level metrics on memory, latency, and throughput. Engineers can now make data-driven decisions on how small they can really go while maintaining practical performance.

Real-world LLM deployment just became significantly more accessible.
