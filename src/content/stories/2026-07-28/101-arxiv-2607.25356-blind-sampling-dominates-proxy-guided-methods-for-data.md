Most data quality pipelines rely on progressive sampling, but which strategy actually works best at scale? This benchmark of nine methods reveals a surprising truth: simple random uniform sampling often crushes more complex, proxy-guided approaches.

For typical data-centric AI pipelines, blind representative samplers achieve near-linear performance (O(N^0.964)) and deliver significantly lower mean relative error (0.49%) compared to methods like DAG-guided MCMC, which can be 11-49 times worse and run super-linearly.

The key takeaway? Representativeness matters more than domain knowledge in sampling for quality profiling. Do not overcomplicate your sampling; a schema-free random uniform approach might be your best bet for production-grade scale.
