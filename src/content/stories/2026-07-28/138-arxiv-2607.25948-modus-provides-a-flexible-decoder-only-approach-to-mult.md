Imagine an AI that can truly speak any language, not just human ones, and understand any form of data, from images to audio to text, and even generate output in any of those forms from any input combination. That is the promise of Modus.

This paper unveils a decoder-only "any-to-any" multimodal architecture that treats all data types symmetrically. Forget separate encoders or heads for different modalities; Modus handles everything through one unified model.

This is a significant shift from traditional encoder-decoder or diffusion models. It opens doors for powerful applications like chained generation (e.g., text to image to 3D model) and even cross-modal self-verification, where the model can score its own outputs by generating an alternative modality. This design could fundamentally change how we build advanced AI agents.
