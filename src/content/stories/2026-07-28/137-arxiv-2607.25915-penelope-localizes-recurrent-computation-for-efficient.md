Complex reasoning tasks with LLMs often demand more computation, usually leading to larger models or longer Chain-of-Thought (CoT) traces. But what if you could localize that extra computation internally, saving tokens and latency?

Penelope introduces a novel latent-reasoning framework for decoder-only Transformers that does just that. It localizes recurrent computation to a specific decoder interval, using time-modulated GRU dynamics and recurrent readout states. This means the model refines its reasoning internally, without verbose intermediate outputs.

A clever "progressive CoT-to-latent curriculum" transfers visible reasoning into this internal path during training. This allows additional computation to be allocated in latent space, achieving competitive accuracy while drastically reducing inference latency compared to generating long visible reasoning traces.

This is a significant step for LLM infrastructure, offering a practical accuracy-efficiency trade-off that can make complex reasoning much more deployable and cost-effective in production.
