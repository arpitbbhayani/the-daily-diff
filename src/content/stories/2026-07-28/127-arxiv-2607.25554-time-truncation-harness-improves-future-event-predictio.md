LLMs struggle with future prediction because current agent frameworks are often external, and data synthesis for temporal reasoning faces "temporal leakage." This paper introduces a groundbreaking "time-truncation harness" to efficiently generate high-quality training data, enabling LLMs to internalize advanced forecasting capabilities.

This harness enforces a temporal cut-off at every turn, mitigating leakage and vastly increasing sampling efficiency for historical events. It allows for TIR-style sampling that produces a broader temporal breadth of search and a higher proportion of quality data, crucial for teaching LLMs to reason over dynamic shifts.

Distillation experiments reveal that models trained with this harness-intervened data achieve superior performance. This is not just an incremental improvement; it is a method for achieving parametric advancement in LLM temporal reasoning. This means building more robust, intrinsically capable AI agents for forecasting.
