Most assume Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) preprocessing is just 'bookkeeping,' but a new study reveals how route-block membership *causally selects* specific packed AWQ arithmetic trajectories on GPUs. This is not a minor detail; it fundamentally impacts LLM inference performance at the lowest hardware levels.

On a Qwen3-Coder AWQ layer, the precise way tokens are aligned into padded expert blocks determines the exact packed quantized matrix multiplication trajectory. Even fixed preconstruction histories can produce distinct native alignments, and injecting opposite alignments transfers entire arithmetic trajectories. This level of mechanism study uncovers hidden performance levers.

For engineers optimizing LLM infrastructure, this means understanding the direct influence of routing on GPU execution paths. This deep insight into the interaction between model architecture, quantization, and GPU specifics is critical for maximizing efficiency and throughput in large-scale AI deployments. It underscores that performance breakthroughs often lie in the most granular details.
