Current LLM benchmarks for dialogue systems often assume static, rational agents, which is far from reality in high-stakes human interactions. DebtBench shatters this illusion by introducing behavioral heterogeneity into negotiation tasks.

This new persona-enriched benchmark for debt collection reveals that most state-of-the-art LLMs struggle with the complexities of diverse human behavior. It is not just about model output, but about nuanced interaction and adapting to different user personas.

For engineers building AI agents that engage with real users, understanding and explicitly testing for behavioral heterogeneity is paramount. This paper offers a much-needed framework and an example agent, DebtGPT, that performs on par with GPT-4o in these challenging scenarios.
