Optimizing Transformer inference, especially with sparse models, is a significant challenge in LLM infrastructure. This paper introduces Ventaglio, a game-changing custom execution unit for vector processors.

Ventaglio leverages RVV ISA extensions to push sparse tensor contractions "at-the-roofline," delivering 6.9-7.4x speedups over optimized RVV baselines. For a pruned LLaMA-3-8B model, it achieves 2.4-5.25x speedup over dense baselines during prefill and autoregressive decoding, respectively, with only 3.1 percent area overhead.

This is not just an incremental improvement; it is a novel hardware-software co-design that fundamentally rethinks how to execute sparse operations efficiently. For any engineer working on scaling LLM inference, understanding these architectural innovations is crucial for unlocking the next level of performance.
