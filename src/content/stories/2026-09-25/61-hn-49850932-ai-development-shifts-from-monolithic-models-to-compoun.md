The AI landscape is undergoing a significant transformation: the focus is shifting from monolithic models to compound AI systems that integrate multiple components.

Leading results, like AlphaCode 2 or AlphaGeometry, are not achieved by bigger, standalone LLMs but by carefully engineered systems. These systems combine LLMs with other tools, filtering, or symbolic solvers to reach state-of-the-art performance.

Databricks reports that 60 percent of LLM applications use Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and 30 percent use multi-step chains, indicating that the future of applied AI is about orchestrating diverse components. This represents a paradigm shift in how we build intelligent applications, emphasizing inference strategies over raw model size.

Understanding this architectural shift is essential for designing robust and capable AI applications.
