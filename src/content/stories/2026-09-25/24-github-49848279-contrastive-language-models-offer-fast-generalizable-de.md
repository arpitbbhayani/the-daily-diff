A new AI model architecture, Contrastive Language Models (CLMs), is here to speed up your agentic workflows by up to 9x while hitting state-of-the-art results on coding benchmarks like Terminal-Bench 2.1 and DeepSWE. This is a significant leap for applied AI.

This 'System One' model achieves its efficiency through a clever design: states and actions are disaggregated, allowing their embeddings to be cached and reused independently. This makes both training and serving dramatically cheaper and faster, addressing a key bottleneck in deploying sophisticated AI agents.

The project is open source and provides a TypeSafe-compatible API, along with fine-tuning tutorials. This means you can plug it into your existing agents and benchmarks to immediately leverage its performance gains and explore its potential for more generalizable decision-making.

You will want to explore this to build more responsive and cost-effective AI systems.
