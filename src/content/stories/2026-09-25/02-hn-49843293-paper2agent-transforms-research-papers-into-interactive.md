Imagine if every research paper could answer your questions, run its own experiments, or explain its methods in plain language. That is the promise of Paper2Agent, a truly innovative framework transforming static research into interactive AI agents.

This system uses multiple agents to analyze a paper and its codebase, building a "model context protocol" (MCP) server. This server then acts as a virtual corresponding author, exposing the paper's manuscript, supplementary materials, datasets, code, and workflows as active, agent-native knowledge.

This is not just about RAG; it is about creating active, executable knowledge systems. It means you could ask an agent based on a genomic paper to interpret variants, or an agent based on a spatial transcriptomics paper to run analyses, all through natural language. This paradigm could revolutionize how we interact with scientific literature and apply domain expertise within our own systems.
