Are you actually getting value from your AI coding assistant? This new tool helps you quantify it by analyzing and replaying your AI coding history locally, revealing exactly where your tokens go and what they cost.

It is not enough to just use AI; you need to manage its consumption. StackReplay helps you see how much you would spend with different AI plans and APIs, offering a concrete way to compare usage against allowances and identify overages.

This is a game-changer for developer productivity, moving beyond anecdotal usage to data-driven optimization. Understand your AI coding habits and make smarter choices for your budget and workflow.
