Are you finding LLMs too slow and costly for structured decision-making in your agentic systems? Jev, a new class of AI models from TypeSafe AI, offers a compelling alternative you need to examine.

Jev is engineered for "System One" tasks, focusing on fast, structured, probabilistic outputs. It boasts claims of being 20-200 times faster and 40-400 times cheaper than general-purpose LLMs for these specific use cases. This is not just incremental improvement, it is a paradigm shift for specific agent workflows.

The article details Jev's core primitives: Choice, Score, and Noul, which allow engineers to define precise questions and receive typed, probabilistic answers directly. Imagine drastically cutting inference costs and latency for critical, high-volume decisions within your AI agents.

This is a must-read for anyone optimizing applied AI and LLM infrastructure for efficiency.
