AI agents are moving from research into production, especially in areas like payments and critical systems. But how do you give them a secure, auditable identity? This project introduces a novel approach using threshold cryptography.

TKeeper allows multiple nodes to collectively hold and authorize an agent's cryptographic key, ensuring no single point of compromise. This framework provides verifiable authorization and a configurable risk model for every action an agent takes.

This is not just about 'security theater.' It is about establishing trust and accountability in autonomous systems, moving AI agents from 'interesting experiment' to 'production-ready participant' in high-stakes operations. A crucial piece of the AI agent infrastructure puzzle.
