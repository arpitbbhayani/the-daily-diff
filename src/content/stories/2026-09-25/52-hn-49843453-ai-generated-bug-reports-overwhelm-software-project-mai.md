Open-source projects like QEMU are facing an unprecedented challenge: a "bugpocalypse" driven by an overwhelming flood of AI-generated bug reports. The volume and increasing plausibility of these reports make distinguishing legitimate issues from AI "slop" incredibly difficult.

This highlights a critical new dimension in engineering practices and developer productivity. Teams must now adapt their triage processes and tooling to cope with potentially hundreds of automatically generated, yet often subtle, bug reports daily.

This article offers frontline insights into how AI agents are already impacting real-world software development workflows, demanding new strategies for maintaining quality and efficiency.
