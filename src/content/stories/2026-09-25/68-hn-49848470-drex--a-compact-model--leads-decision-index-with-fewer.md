A new small AI model called Drex is challenging the assumption that bigger models always mean better decisions. This model, under 10 billion parameters, directly outputs probabilities for options, skipping token-heavy text parsing entirely.

Drex has demonstrated superior performance on specialized decision-making tasks against models with 12 to 36 billion parameters. It excels in areas like contract analysis, tool call routing, and complex judgment tasks by focusing purely on the decision output.

This compact, efficient design is a game-changer for building faster, more reliable AI agents where quick, structured decisions are paramount. It shows that specialized, optimized architectures can significantly outperform larger general-purpose models in specific domains.

The future of efficient AI decisions is not always about scale.
