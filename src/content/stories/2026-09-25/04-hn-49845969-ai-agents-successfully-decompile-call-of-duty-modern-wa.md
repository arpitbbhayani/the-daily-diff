Imagine an AI system tackling the monumental task of decompiling a complex commercial game. One engineer did exactly that, deploying a multi-agent setup with Claude to convert Modern Warfare 2 into C++ and reporting on a month of intensive, token-heavy progress.

This experiment is a masterclass in applied AI agents. It details a four-agent workflow, orchestrated with Discord and GitHub webhooks, and augmented with industry-standard tools like Ghidra and IDA Pro. The results are concrete: 7,000 commits and 34 percent of functions decompiled.

This is not just theoretical. It demonstrates real-world orchestration challenges and provides invaluable insights into managing context, coordinating tasks, and integrating LLMs with specialized tools to solve a problem of significant complexity. You will gain a clear picture of how practical multi-agent systems can be built and evaluated.
