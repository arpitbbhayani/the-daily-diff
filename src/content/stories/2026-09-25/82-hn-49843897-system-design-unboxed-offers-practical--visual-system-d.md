You know the feeling: needing deep system design knowledge but finding either overly theoretical textbooks or superficial blog posts. "System Design Unboxed" aims to bridge that gap with a highly practical approach.

The book offers 12 complete system designs across 17 chapters, focusing on production patterns and clear architectural diagrams. What sets it apart are the editable diagrams included in Mermaid, SVG, and draw.io formats, allowing you to adapt and learn interactively. 

This resource looks like a superb way to learn and internalize scalable system concepts, moving beyond rote memorization to truly understanding the trade-offs and implementations for real-world scenarios. It is an excellent addition to any senior engineer's reference library.
