You are likely spending too much on LLM inference in your AI agents. Basic prompt caching, often overlooked, can cut these costs dramatically.

A company that ran production data agents saw a 90 percent reduction in spend by focusing on this simple, yet often misapplied, technique. This is not about complex algorithms; it is about smart management of repetitive LLM calls.

Learning to identify and cache common prompts is an immediate win for anyone building or deploying AI agents. It ensures that you are not paying for the same computation multiple times, freeing up budget for more complex and novel agent behaviors.
