Imagine a bug tracker that lives entirely within your Git repository, distributed, offline-first, and immune to vendor lock-in. That is exactly what Git-bug offers, radically rethinking how teams manage issues.

It leverages Git's inherent distribution model, allowing you to push and pull bugs just like code. This means full offline capability, ensuring you can access and update issues whether you are on a plane or without internet access, and eliminating the worry of external service outages.

This approach offers significant advantages for security-conscious or distributed teams. It removes external dependencies, simplifies backups, and integrates seamlessly with existing Git workflows, enhancing developer productivity and control over your issue data.
