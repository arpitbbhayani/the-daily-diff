Imagine practicing system design interviews not just by sketching diagrams, but by having an AI ruthlessly challenge your trade-offs. This new tool does exactly that, going beyond simple feedback to truly stress-test your architectural reasoning.

It is one thing to propose a solution, but another to defend it against a critical interviewer. This AI focuses on forcing you to articulate why you chose a specific database, why that caching strategy, or why your scaling approach makes sense given the constraints. This direct, adversarial practice helps uncover blind spots.

For senior engineers, this is not just about interview prep; it is about sharpening your decision-making and learning to proactively justify complex system choices. Think of it as a sparring partner for your brain.
