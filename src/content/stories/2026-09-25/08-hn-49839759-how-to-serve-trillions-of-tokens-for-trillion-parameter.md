Serving trillion-parameter coding agents requires handling trillions of tokens, a monumental challenge in LLM infrastructure. This article dives deep into the performance demands for economically viable inference, operating at near hardware peak rates.

The key insight is the need for extreme scale to amortize hardware and engineering costs. This means going beyond basic model serving to optimize every aspect, from matrix math accelerators to data movement and memory management, all while maintaining high throughput and low latency.

You will learn how to design and implement highly optimized LLM inference services. This is not just about throwing more GPUs at the problem, but about rethinking the entire system architecture to achieve unprecedented scale and efficiency for the next generation of AI agents.
