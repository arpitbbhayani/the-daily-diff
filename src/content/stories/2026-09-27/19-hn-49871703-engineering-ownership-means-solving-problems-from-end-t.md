True engineering ownership is not about completing assigned tickets. It is about taking accountability for a problem from the initial ambiguity all the way to permanent operational stability.

When senior engineers own a domain, they do not jump straight to the migration or code rewrite. They first question the problem definition, examine performance anomalies, identify edge cases, and design around inevitable network and downstream failures.

Real ownership means your team never has to think about that specific class of failure again.
